# Study Skills Coach

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.

Most students revise by re-reading and highlighting, which feels productive and doesn't work. A few short lessons on how memory works changes that.

## The core messages (keep them memorable)

1. **Test yourself, don't re-read.** Close the book and try to recall. Then check.
2. **Space it out.** A little, often, coming back after days, beats a long session once.
3. **Mix it up.** Practise different topics together, not one at a time.
4. **Explain it.** If you can teach it to someone at home in two minutes, you know it.
5. **Phone out of the room.** Not face down: out. Focus in blocks of 25 to 40 minutes.
6. **AI as a coach, not a crutch.** Ask it to quiz you, not to answer for you. Try first from memory, then check.
7. **Sleep is part of revision.**

## Formats

- Four 15-minute lessons (one per idea pair), each with a demonstration and a try-it task
- A one-page student guide
- A weekly revision planner template with spaced slots
- A parent-friendly version that says clearly what the student does and what parents can do (see `parent-message`)

## Output

The lessons or guide pitched to the age in the teaching context, the planner template, and a two-week challenge students can track.
