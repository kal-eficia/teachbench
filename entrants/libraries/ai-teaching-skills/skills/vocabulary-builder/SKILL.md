# Vocabulary Builder

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.

Students lose marks for knowing the idea but not the word. Vocabulary needs teaching on purpose, then using again and again.

## Choose the words

- **Tier 3** (subject-specific): *volatile, photosynthesis, alliteration, denominator*. These are the exam words.
- **Tier 2** (academic, across subjects): *evaluate, significant, function, process, infer*. Often assumed, often not known.
- Pick 6 to 10 per unit. More than that and none stick.

## For each word

1. **Say it** (with pronunciation if tricky) and have students say it.
2. **Student-friendly definition**, then the exam definition if it differs.
3. **Word parts** where they help: *photo* (light) + *synthesis* (putting together).
4. **Two examples and a non-example.**
5. **Confusions**: words it's mixed up with (*affect / effect*, *CSS / CSV*).
6. **Use it**: students write a sentence using the word correctly about this topic.

## Keep it alive

- Put the words in starters and quizzes for the next six weeks (see `recall-starter`).
- Insist on the word in answers: "Say that again using *volatile*."
- Display them, but test them; display alone doesn't teach.

## Output

A vocabulary table (word, tier, friendly definition, exam definition, word parts, example, non-example, confusion), a 10-minute teaching activity, and a retrieval schedule for the next six weeks.
