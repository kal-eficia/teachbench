# Mentoring Session Planner

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.
>
> **Student data:** use initials or numbers instead of names, and leave out health, SEND diagnoses, safeguarding and family details unless your school's data protection and AI policy allow this tool to handle them. If you can't tell, anonymise first.

Mentoring works when the student does most of the talking and leaves with one small action they chose.

## Session shape (20 to 30 minutes)

1. **Check-in (3 min)**: how things are going, in their words.
2. **Review (5 min)**: last time's action. What happened? What helped or got in the way? No judgement.
3. **Focus (10 to 15 min)**: one area only (a subject, organisation, revision, motivation, a goal).
4. **Action (5 min)**: one specific, small step they choose, with when and how. "I'll do 15 minutes of flashcards after Asr on Monday and Wednesday" beats "revise more".
5. **Close (2 min)**: what they're taking away; when you'll meet next.

## Coaching questions

- "What's going well that we can build on?"
- "On a scale of 1 to 10, how confident are you about X? What would make it one point higher?"
- "What have you tried? What happened?"
- "What's one thing you could do this week?"
- "What might get in the way, and what will you do then?"

## Rules

- Listen more than advise. Offer ideas only after asking for theirs.
- Celebrate progress against their own baseline, not other students.
- If something raises a safeguarding or wellbeing concern, follow the school's procedure.

## Output

The session plan with questions tailored to the student's focus, and a one-line record template: date, focus, action, follow-up.
