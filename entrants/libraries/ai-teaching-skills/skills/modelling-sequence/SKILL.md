# Modelling Sequence

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.

Students can't do what they've never seen done. Modelling makes an expert's invisible thinking visible, then hands it over a piece at a time.

## I do (teacher models)

- Choose a task like the one students will do, but not identical.
- Write a **think-aloud script**: what the teacher says while working. Include the decisions ("I'll start with the effect because the question says *explain*"), the checks ("Does this link back to the question?") and a deliberate small mistake that's then caught and fixed.
- Keep it under 8 minutes.

## We do (guided practice)

- A second task, completed together. Students suggest each step; the teacher questions and refines.
- Use a structure students can see: a success-criteria checklist, sentence starters, a writing frame or a flow chart of steps.
- Check understanding before releasing (see `hinge-question-writer`).

## You do (independent practice)

- A third task, done alone, with the scaffold available but not forced.
- Then a fourth, without the scaffold.
- Students check their work against the success criteria before submitting.

## Output

- The model task and full think-aloud script
- The we-do task with the teacher's prompts
- The you-do tasks, the scaffold, and a model answer for each
- The success criteria, written for students
