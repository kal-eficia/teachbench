# SEND Adaptations

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.
>
> **Student data:** use initials or numbers instead of names, and leave out health, SEND diagnoses, safeguarding and family details unless your school's data protection and AI policy allow this tool to handle them. If you can't tell, anonymise first.

Good adaptations remove barriers to the learning, not the learning itself. Most also help every other student in the room.

## Ask first (briefly)

The specific need and what the student's support plan or SENCO advice says. Never diagnose; work from what the teacher tells you. Keep individual details out of anything shared.

## Adaptation menu (choose what fits the barrier)

**Reading and processing (e.g. dyslexia)**
- Clean layout, sans-serif font, 12 to 14 pt, 1.5 line spacing, left-aligned, no walls of text
- Instructions numbered, one step per line; key words in bold, defined in a box
- Text read aloud or provided as audio; reduced copying from the board

**Attention (e.g. ADHD)**
- Tasks chunked into 5 to 10 minute pieces with a visible checklist
- Clear start and end for each task; movement or a role where possible
- Fewer, more important questions rather than many

**Social communication and predictability (e.g. autism)**
- The lesson structure shown at the start; warnings before changes
- Literal, unambiguous instructions; no idioms without explanation
- Group work with defined roles, or an option to work alone

**Motor and organisation (e.g. dyspraxia)**
- Typed or verbal answers allowed; templates with boxes for answers
- Extra time for practical tasks; equipment checklists

**Memory**
- Knowledge organisers to hand, worked examples kept visible, frequent short retrieval

## Keep the challenge

Same objective, same key questions. Change the route (scaffold, format, chunking), not the destination. Plan when each scaffold is removed.

## Output

The adapted resource, a short note on what changed and why, and when to fade each support.
