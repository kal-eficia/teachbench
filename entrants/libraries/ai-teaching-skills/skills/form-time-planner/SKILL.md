# Form Time Planner

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.

Form time is 15 to 20 minutes most days, and it adds up to weeks of the year. Use it with purpose, and keep it light enough to run every day.

## A weekly rhythm (adapt)

- **Monday: organisation**: planners, the week's deadlines, one study habit
- **Tuesday: reading**: a short article or extract, two discussion questions
- **Wednesday: current events**: one story, explained neutrally, "what do you think and why?"
- **Thursday: futures**: a career, a pathway, a skill employers want
- **Friday: reflection and recognition**: wins of the week, one thing to improve

## Rules for activities

- Inclusive and respectful of the school's values and faiths; check the teaching context for any house rules (for example, no games built on deliberately false statements).
- Nobody is put on the spot about personal matters. Wellbeing topics are general, with signposting to who students can talk to.
- Short, clear instructions; works with or without screens.
- Mix individual, pair and whole-group moments.

## Output

A term of sessions (one line each), the full plan for the next two weeks (aim, activity, prompts, timing), and any resources to print or display.
