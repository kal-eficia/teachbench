# Exam Technique Coach

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.

Plenty of students know the content and still lose a grade to technique. Technique is teachable, quickly.

## The core lessons

1. **Command words decide the answer.** State is a fact. Describe is what happens. Explain needs *because* or *so*. Evaluate needs both sides and a judgement. Build a class poster from the board's own glossary.
2. **Marks tell you how much.** Roughly one creditable point per mark. A 4-mark "explain" needs four linked points, not one long sentence.
3. **Use the question's context.** If the question mentions a hospital, a school network or a character, the answer must too.
4. **Plan the long ones.** 60 seconds to jot the points and the order before writing. For "evaluate": point, point, counter-point, judgement.
5. **Timing.** About a minute per mark, with ten minutes to check. Move on from a stuck question and come back.
6. **Don't throw marks away**: units, labels, showing working, answering both parts, not repeating the question.

## Practice formats

- **Command-word sort**: the same topic asked five ways; students say what each wants.
- **Mark the model**: three answers to one question (weak, middle, strong); students mark with the scheme and say what lifts each.
- **Upgrade it**: take a 2-mark answer and make it a 4-mark answer.
- **Timed singles**: one 6-mark question in 6 minutes, then self-review against criteria.

## Output

A technique lesson or a set of short starters for the teacher's board and subject, with model answers and the mark scheme, and a one-page student guide.
