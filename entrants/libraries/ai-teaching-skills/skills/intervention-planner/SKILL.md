# Intervention Planner

> If `teaching-context.md` exists, read it first, follow its house rules, and don't ask for anything it already answers.
>
> **Student data:** use initials or numbers instead of names, and leave out health, SEND diagnoses, safeguarding and family details unless your school's data protection and AI policy allow this tool to handle them. If you can't tell, anonymise first.

Interventions work when they're short, specific and measured. "Maths booster for Year 11" is a hope. "Six sessions on fractions to percentages for these eight students, measured by the same 10-question check" is a plan.

## Steps

1. **Choose students from evidence**: question-level analysis, diagnostics or quiz data (see `question-level-analysis`). Group by the same gap, not the same grade.
2. **Name the gap precisely**: one skill or misconception per intervention.
3. **Set the measure first**: a short check given at the start and the end.
4. **Plan the sessions**: 4 to 8 sessions of 20 to 40 minutes. Each: retrieval, explicit teaching, worked examples, practice, exit check.
5. **Timing**: when it happens, so students don't miss the same lesson every week.
6. **Exit criteria**: the score or evidence that means a student is done; others continue or are re-grouped.
7. **Communicate**: a short, positive message to students and parents (see `parent-message`).

## Output

The intervention plan (group, gap, measure, sessions, timing, exit criteria), the session materials outline, the pre and post check, and a one-paragraph summary for a line manager.
