# Including a new arrival (Dari and Pashto speaker) in Year 9 photosynthesis

**Assumptions:** she can read and write in Dari script, you have a bilingual dictionary or a translation app, and there is a friendly classmate who can sit beside her. The photosynthesis lesson is a standard KS3 one: plants make glucose using light, carbon dioxide and water, and release oxygen.

**The main idea:** she has already studied science, so the barrier is English, not ability. Support the language and keep the thinking at full Year 9 level. Do not give her a simpler topic or colouring-in.

---

## 1. Before the lesson (10 minutes of your time)

- **Find out what she knows.** Show her the word equation and the symbol equation, 6CO2 + 6H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. Chemical symbols and equations look the same in every language, so she may recognise them straight away. Use a translation app to ask her, in Dari, what the equation means and what she remembers about plants. Let her answer in Dari and translate it back.
- **Send her the vocabulary sheet below the day before** so she can look it up at home and ask family to help.
- **Check translations with a fluent speaker.** The words below are my best attempt and Afghan Dari can differ from Iranian Persian, so ask her, a family member or a school translator to confirm before you print them.

---

## 2. Vocabulary sheet (pre-teach these 8 words)

| English | Picture to draw or show | Simple meaning | Dari (please check) |
|---|---|---|---|
| photosynthesis | plant with arrows | how plants make food using light | فتوسنتز |
| leaf | green leaf | the part of a plant that catches light | برگ |
| sunlight | sun with rays | light from the Sun | نور آفتاب |
| carbon dioxide | CO2 with arrow into leaf | a gas in the air that plants take in | دی اکسید کاربن |
| water | drop going up the root | what plants take in through roots | آب |
| glucose | sugar cube | the sugar plants make for energy | گلوکوز |
| oxygen | O2 with arrow out of leaf | a gas plants give out | اکسیجن |
| chlorophyll | green dots in a leaf | the green stuff that catches light | کلوروفیل |

---

## 3. Simplify instructions, not science

Give her the same task as everyone else, but write each instruction on its own line, with the same verbs every time.

1. Look at the diagram.
2. Write the **inputs** (what goes in): ________
3. Write the **outputs** (what comes out): ________
4. Write the equation.
5. Explain: "Plants need light because ________."

Keep the verbs the same lesson after lesson: look, write, label, explain, compare.

---

## 4. Sentence frames

For describing: "A plant takes in ________ and ________."
For explaining: "Plants need ________ because ________."
For cause and result: "If there is no light, the plant cannot ________."
For comparing: "Photosynthesis makes ________, but respiration uses ________."

---

## 5. During the lesson

- **Use diagrams as the main teaching tool.** A labelled diagram with arrows (light, CO2, water in; glucose, O2 out) carries most of the meaning without words.
- **Talk before writing.** Pair her with a supportive classmate. They use the frames aloud, then she writes. Don't make her speak in front of the whole class yet.
- **Let her use Dari.** She can annotate the diagram, take notes and plan her answer in Dari first, then build the English version. This keeps her thinking at full level.
- **Check she understands the question,** not only the content. Point to the command word (explain, describe, compare) and make sure she knows what it asks for.
- **Practical or demonstration.** If you do a pondweed bubbling or leaf-starch test, she can take part fully, and watching is language-light.

---

## 6. After the lesson

- Give her a **short quiz with labelling and equation completion** first, so she can show what she knows without a lot of writing. Then add one sentence-frame question.
- **Mark the science, not the spelling.** Note language errors separately and don't penalise them.
- Do not put her in a lower set or give lower-level work. Her progress will come quickly as vocabulary builds.

---

## 7. Which support to fade first

1. The translations on the vocabulary sheet (after about three lessons, once the key words are secure).
2. The sentence frames (when she begins using them without looking).
3. The pair partner (last, because it is also good for her social settling in).

Keep the labelled diagrams for everyone, because they help the whole class.
