# Year 8 French homework: this week

**Time:** 25 minutes, in three short chunks. No AI, dictionary or translation app (the point is to find out what you can do from memory). **Parents: you don't need any French.** See the box at the end.

*Assumptions: "perfect tense with avoir" covered regular -er verbs (j'ai joué), probably regular -ir and -re (j'ai fini, j'ai vendu), and a few common irregulars (j'ai fait, j'ai eu). The task is built so it still works if you have only covered -er verbs: the stretch is marked.*

---

## Part 1: From memory (5 minutes)

Cover the box at the bottom of the page. Write these **from memory**, then check.

1. *avoir* in the present, all six forms: **j'** ___, **tu** ___, **il/elle/on** ___, **nous** ___, **vous** ___, **ils/elles** ___
2. Turn these verbs into their "-ed" part (the past participle): jouer → ___, manger → ___, regarder → ___, écouter → ___
3. Say what each means in English: **mon père, ma sœur, mes grands-parents, mon oncle**
4. Say in French: *I like, I hate, because, boring, funny*

## Part 2: Past tense practice (10 minutes)

**A. Fill the gaps with the right form of *avoir* + the verb in brackets.**

1. Hier, je ___ (regarder) un film.
2. Samedi, mon père ___ (manger) une pizza.
3. Nous ___ (jouer) au football.
4. Mes parents ___ (acheter) un cadeau.
5. Tu ___ (écouter) la musique ?
6. Ma sœur ___ (finir) ses devoirs.

**B. One of these is wrong. Find it and fix it.**

- J'ai regardé la télé.
- Mon frère a mangé du pain.
- Nous avons joué au tennis.
- Ma mère ai acheté un livre.

**C. Stretch (optional): irregular verbs.** Fill the gaps with *fait* (made/did), *eu* (had) or *vu* (saw).

1. Hier, mon oncle a ___ un gâteau.
2. Ma cousine a ___ un chat noir.
3. J'ai ___ mon ami au parc.

## Part 3: Write and say (10 minutes)

Write **four sentences** about what *your family did yesterday or at the weekend*. Each one needs:

- a family member (mon frère, ma mère, mes cousins...)
- *avoir* + a past participle
- **one** of your four sentences must give an opinion with *parce que*

**Frame to help you:**

> Hier / Samedi / Dimanche, **mon frère** a **joué** au foot. J'aime ça **parce que** c'est amusant.

Then **say your four sentences out loud**, slowly, to someone at home. It can be a parent, a sibling, a pet, or the mirror.

---

## Check your answers (do this *after* you've tried everything)

**Part 1:** 1. j'ai, tu as, il/elle/on a, nous avons, vous avez, ils/elles ont. 2. joué, mangé, regardé, écouté. 3. my father, my sister, my grandparents, my uncle. 4. j'aime, je déteste, parce que, ennuyeux, amusant (drôle is also fine).

**Part 2A:** 1. ai regardé. 2. a mangé. 3. avons joué. 4. ont acheté. 5. as écouté. 6. a fini.
**2B:** "Ma mère ai acheté" is wrong. It should be "Ma mère **a** acheté un livre." (*ma mère* = she, so we use *a*.)
**2C:** 1. fait. 2. eu. 3. vu.

**Part 3:** Tick each sentence if it has a family member, *a/ai/as/avons/avez/ont* + a participle, and one has *parce que*.

## For parents and carers (2 minutes, no French needed)

Your child will say four French sentences out loud. **All you need to do** is listen, count to four, and tick the box on their planner or sheet: "Said 4 sentences aloud: ✓". If they get stuck, ask them to look at the answer section above, or to try again more slowly. That's it.

*Teacher note:* Quick check next lesson: ask students to read one sentence aloud (cold-call, 3 minutes). Collect Part 3 for a quick tick-and-glance or use whole-class feedback on the commonest error (usually forgetting *avoir* or using *être* by mistake). Optional extension: spot-check whether students used *a* with singular and *ont* with plural subjects.
