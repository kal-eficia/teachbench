## How you can help at home
Times tables stick best with a little practice, often. Five minutes a day is plenty.

- **Your child:** says the 6, 7 or 9 times table out loud, in order first, then jumbled up ("What is 7 times 6?").
- **You:** listen, say "again" if they get stuck, and celebrate the ones they get right. You don't need to teach anything.
- **Fun one for the 9s:** ask your child to show you the finger trick, or to explain why the digits of every answer add up to 9.
