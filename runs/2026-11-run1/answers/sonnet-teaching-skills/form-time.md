# Year 7 form time: first term, four weeks (15 minutes a day)

**Assumptions:** this is weeks 3 to 6 of the first term, once registers and routines are settled. Pupils are 11 to 12, there are about 25 to 30 in the form, and the school has its own phone policy. The sessions below don't depend on screens, and nobody is asked to share personal stories or answer in front of the group. Check the phone activities against your school policy.

## Principles for all 20 sessions

- **Same shape every day (15 minutes):** 2 minutes settle and register, 10 minutes main activity, 3 minutes "one thing I'll do". The routine means pupils always know what to expect.
- **Nobody is put on the spot about personal matters.** Use "a Year 7 called Sam" scenarios, anonymous cards and pair talk first, then whole-group share, and let pupils say "pass".
- **No tricks or false statements.** Every statement pupils see or say is true. Scenarios are labelled "imagine" and are clearly hypothetical.
- **End each week with the same Friday routine:** a celebration, plus one thing to improve next week.
- **Signpost.** Say each week who pupils can talk to (you, the head of year, the pastoral lead, a trusted adult) and where.

## The four-week overview

| Week | Theme | Big question | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Organisation** | "How do I stop forgetting things?" | Planner and the week ahead | Pack the bag: the night-before routine | Where does my homework go? (tracking system) | Fix a messy bag (pairs) | Celebrate: one thing I did on time |
| 2 | **Friendships** | "What does a good friend do?" | What makes a good friend? (qualities, not names) | Joining in: how to start talking to someone new | When friends fall out: what next? | Kind words: saying no and saying sorry | Compliment cards |

## Full plan: weeks 1 and 2

### Week 1: Organisation

**Aim for the week:** each pupil has one working system for planner, bag and homework.

**Mon: Planner and the week ahead (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle. Question on the board: "What's one thing you've forgotten this term?" (talk to a partner, no sharing)
- 8 min: pupils open planners (or use a printed week grid). Model with the class: write one lesson's homework with the date set and the date due. Pupils fill in this week.
- 3 min: **Today's one thing:** "Check my planner when I get home."
- Resource: printed weekly grid for pupils with no planner.

**Tue: The night-before routine (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 8 min: as a class, build a routine on the board: homework done, bag packed, PE kit, planner, charged devices for lessons if needed. Pupils write their own 5-step list on a card to keep in the bag.
- 3 min: pupils pair up and read each other's lists. "Is there anything to add?"
- **Today's one thing:** do step one tonight.

**Wed: Where does my homework go? (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 8 min: show the system (folder, tray, or digital space). "Three places homework can be: in my bag, in a folder, in the right tray." Pupils sort their bag contents into **keep, file and bin** (physical clear-out, 5 minutes).
- 3 min: tell a partner where you'll put your homework from now on.

**Thu: Fix a messy bag (pairs) (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 9 min: pairs get a card describing a pretend messy bag ("a crumpled worksheet, no pen, a PE kit, a lunch box that's leaking"). Their task: write 3 things "Sam" should change. Share 2 or 3 pairs.
- 3 min: pupils pick one change to make themselves.
- Note: the scenarios are clearly imagined, so no pupil is shamed.

**Fri: Celebrate (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 8 min: pupils write on a sticky note one thing they did on time this week. Stick on the board. Read a selection aloud (with consent).
- 5 min: "Next week I'll improve ____." Pupils complete the sentence privately.

### Week 2: Friendships

**Aim for the week:** pupils can describe what a good friend does, can start a conversation, and know what to do when friends fall out.

**Mon: What makes a good friend? (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 8 min: pupils write 3 qualities of a good friend on separate cards (kind, loyal, listens, fun). Pairs rank them, then the class builds one list on the board.
- 3 min: "Which of these am I good at?" Pupils note it privately.
- No names, and no one is listed as a friend or non-friend.

**Tue: Joining in (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 9 min: as a class, brainstorm "things I can say to join a group" (e.g. "Can I join in?", "What are you playing?"). Pairs practise two lines in a role-play with an imagined new pupil.
- 3 min: pick one line you'll try this week.
- Note: reassure pupils that feeling nervous about this is normal for adults too.

**Wed: When friends fall out (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 9 min: read a short imagined scenario ("Two friends have a disagreement about a game at break"). In threes: what could each person do next? Choose from "talk it out", "take a break", "ask for help". Use "I feel... when..." sentence stems.
- 3 min: remind pupils who they can go to.

**Thu: Kind words (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 9 min: practise polite ways of saying no ("No thanks, I'd rather not") and saying sorry ("I'm sorry I did that, can we start again?"). Pairs rehearse.
- 3 min: "One way I can be kind today is..."

**Fri: Compliment cards (15 min)**
- 2 min: settle.
- 9 min: every pupil gets a card with a classmate's name drawn from a bag by you (so everyone gets one). They write one true, specific, kind sentence. Check each card before handing out, to make sure nobody is left out or hurt.
- 4 min: pupils read their cards silently, then say "thank you" to the writer.

## Weeks 3 and 4: what happens in each session

### Week 3: Phones and screens

- **Mon, My day in screens:** pupils guess how many hours they spend on screens in a typical day, then work it out for yesterday on a simple grid (private, no sharing totals). "Anything surprise you?"
- **Tue, Notifications:** short class talk about how apps are designed to hold attention (notifications, streaks, autoplay). Pupils list three things on a phone that pull at them. Question: "Who benefits when I keep scrolling?"
- **Wed, Group chats:** discuss with an imagined group chat scenario: someone is left out or someone posts something unkind. Pupils decide what a good friend would do, and who to tell. Remind of school rules and where to report.
- **Thu, Sleep and screens:** a short, factual explanation of why screens close to bedtime can make it harder to switch off. Pupils choose one change (phone charges outside the bedroom, screen off 30 minutes before bed).
- **Fri, Phone-free challenge plan:** pupils design a small experiment (for example, no phone during homework this week). Write a "what I'll try" sentence, which is theirs to keep.

### Week 4: Putting it together

- **Mon, What's working?** Pupils look back through their one-thing sentences. Traffic light: green (keeping it up), amber (sometimes), red (needs help).
- **Tue, A weekly plan:** pupils fill a simple grid of homework, clubs, rest and family time. Check it's realistic.
- **Wed, Trusted adult map:** pupils draw "people I can talk to": at school, at home and outside. Private; no sharing needed.
- **Thu, Phone agreement:** pupils draft three personal rules for phone use at home ("phone off at 9", "no phone at dinner"). A parent-friendly version they can show at home if they choose.
- **Fri, Celebration and review:** pupils write what they've improved in one sentence, and one thing they're still working on. Collect a short anonymous note to see what the form wants next.

## Notes for you

- If a pupil becomes upset during a friendship session, follow your usual pastoral route and see them privately.
- Most sessions need no screens. Where it says "grid", print it.
- If you want a home link, send parents a short message at the end of week 4 with the three phone rules their child drafted, and make clear it is the child's plan, not homework for parents.
