# Feedback on your personal statement opening

**Where it sits now:** Clear but generic. Many applicants begin with almost these exact words, so the opening doesn't yet show an admissions tutor who you are.

**Strengths**
- Your motivation is sincere and easy to follow: "I want to make a difference."
- It is short and direct, so nothing needs cutting, only deepening.

**Your priority: replace claims with evidence.** "Passionate about helping people", "I love science" and "hard-working and dedicated" are things you tell the reader. Nothing is shown. Tutors want a moment, not a mood. Look at your first sentence: "Ever since I was a little girl" can't be checked, and it is a very common way to start. Also, helping people is true of many careers, so ask what is specifically medical about what drew you.

**Accuracy notes:** "Ever since... I have always" says the same thing twice. "Perfect career" is vague, so say what you actually know about the work.

**Rewrite task:** Redraft the opening in three or four sentences. Start with one specific experience (volunteering, shadowing, a book, a conversation) and end with what it taught you about medicine. Don't begin any sentence with "I am."
