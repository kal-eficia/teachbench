# Year 10 RS (AQA, Christianity and Islam): Teaching Key Terms So They Stick

**The problem:** students know the idea but lose marks because they don't use the word, or they use it wrongly. So the plan is to teach six words on purpose, make students say and write them, then bring them back for six weeks.

**Key idea:** pair each Christian term with an Islamic one so students compare and don't blur them.

| Pair | Christianity | Islam | The contrast to teach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trinity | Tawhid | One God in three persons vs the oneness of God |
| 2 | Incarnation | Risalah | God becoming human vs God guiding through human prophets |
| 3 | Atonement | Akhirah | How people are put right with God vs what happens after death |

## The vocabulary table

| Word | Say it | Tier | Student-friendly definition | Exam-style definition | Word parts | Example | Non-example | Common confusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tawhid** | tow-HEED | 3 | God is one. Only one God, nobody and nothing shares his power. | The oneness of Allah. | From Arabic *wahid* (one). | "Muslims say the Shahadah because it declares tawhid." | Worshipping idols or saying God has partners (this is *shirk*). | Mixed up with the Trinity: "Muslims believe in three gods" (wrong). Also with "monotheism" in general. |
| **Akhirah** | AH-khi-rah (the *kh* is like *loch*) | 3 | Life after death, and the belief that everyone will be judged by Allah. | Belief in life after death, the Day of Judgement, and heaven (Jannah) or hell (Jahannam). | From Arabic *akhir* (last, final). | "Because of akhirah, a Muslim may give to charity, knowing they are accountable." | Reincarnation (coming back as something else). | "Akhirah" and "afterlife" used as if it's only heaven. It also includes judgement. |
| **Risalah** | ri-SAH-lah | 3 | Allah sent messages to people through prophets, ending with Muhammad. | Prophethood: the belief that Allah guided humanity through prophets. | From Arabic root for "to send"; a *rasul* is a messenger. | "Risalah shows Allah did not leave people without guidance." | The Qur'an itself (that is the message, not the belief in prophethood). | "Risalah is the holy book." Wrong: it's the idea of prophets and messages. |
| **Incarnation** | in-kar-NAY-shun | 3 | God became a human being in Jesus. | The belief that God took human form in Jesus Christ. | *in* (into) + *carn* (flesh): "made flesh". Same root as carnivore. | "John 1:14: 'the Word became flesh'." | Reincarnation. Resurrection (Jesus coming back to life). | Incarnation vs resurrection vs reincarnation. |
| **Atonement** | a-TONE-ment | 3 | Jesus' death on the cross put humans right with God, making forgiveness possible. | The belief that Jesus' sacrifice reconciles people with God. | *at-one-ment*: being "at one" with God again. | "Christians believe the atonement shows God's love, because Jesus died for sinners." | Saying sorry in church (that is confession). | Mixed up with salvation, forgiveness, or resurrection. |
| **Trinity** | TRIN-i-tee | 3 | One God, three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. | God as three persons in one being. | *tri* (three) + *unity*. | "The Trinity is shown at Jesus' baptism: the Father's voice, the Son, the Spirit like a dove." | "Three gods." | "Christians worship three gods" (wrong: it's one God). |

*(Check the wording against your AQA specification and the textbook you use, and adjust where they differ.)*

## Teaching sequence

**Lesson 1: Trinity and Tawhid.**
1. Say it: choral repetition three times, then pair-say.
2. Short explanation with a diagram of each belief.
3. Students complete a Frayer card for each word: own-words definition, example, non-example, a sentence.
4. **10-minute activity: "Fix the sentence."** Show errors and ask students to correct them using the right term:
   - "Christians believe in three gods called the Trinity."
   - "Tawhid means Muslims believe there are many gods."
5. Exit ticket: write one sentence using each word correctly.

**Lessons 2 and 3:** the same routine for the other two pairs (Incarnation and Risalah; Atonement and Akhirah). More misconception sentences:
- "The incarnation is when Jesus came back to life." (resurrection)
- "Risalah is the Muslim holy book." (Qur'an)
- "Akhirah is when your soul is born again as an animal." (reincarnation)
- "The atonement is when Christians say sorry in church." (confession)

## Using words correctly in exam answers

Teach one frame and use it every time:

> **[Term]** means ______. Christians/Muslims believe this because ______. This affects how they ______.

Students then use it in 4-mark and 5-mark explain questions, and in the "evaluate" arguments.
- **Insist on the word.** When a student says "Muslims believe in one God", respond: "Say that again using *tawhid*."
- **Mark for it.** On practice answers, circle each key term and write ✓ or ✗ for correct use.
- **Display the words but test them.** Displaying alone doesn't teach.

## Six-week retrieval schedule

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Same day | Exit ticket: sentence for each word |
| Next lesson | 3-question starter on yesterday's pair |
| End of week 1 | Mixed quiz: define, odd one out, fix the sentence |
| Week 2 | Starter: "Which term fits?" with short scenarios |
| Week 3 | Compare task: "Explain the difference between the Trinity and tawhid" |
| Week 4 | Include terms in a practice 5-mark answer; mark term use |
| Week 6 | 10-question vocabulary quiz covering all six; re-teach any below 70% |

Keep a note of which terms get confused most, and weight the next starters towards them.

*Assumptions: AQA GCSE Religious Studies A, about three lessons a week, mixed-attainment class, and a note that your Muslim students may know some of these words well already, so invite them to explain.*
