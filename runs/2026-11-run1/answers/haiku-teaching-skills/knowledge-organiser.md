# Knowledge Organiser: The Norman Conquest, 1066

## 1. The three claimants

1. **Harold Godwinson**, Earl of Wessex. Crowned king in January 1066, after Edward the Confessor died without a clear heir. The Witan chose him.
2. **William, Duke of Normandy.** Claimed that Edward had promised him the throne.
3. **Harald Hardrada**, King of Norway. Invaded in September 1066 with Tostig Godwinson, Harold's brother, and claimed the English throne.

## 2. 1066 timeline

- **January 1066:** Edward dies. Harold is crowned.
- **September 1066:** Hardrada and Tostig invade the north.
- **25 September:** Battle of Stamford Bridge. Harold defeats the invaders.
- **28 September:** William lands at Pevensey, in Sussex.
- **14 October:** Battle of Hastings. William wins, and Harold is killed.
- **25 December:** William is crowned king in Westminster Abbey.

## 3. The two battles

- **Stamford Bridge:** Harold's army marches north and wins, then has to rush south.
- **Hastings:** The English fight on foot in a shield wall on a hill. Norman archers and cavalry (mounted soldiers) win the day.

## 4. How William kept control

1. **Castles:** Motte and bailey castles (a wooden tower on a mound, with an enclosed yard) were built quickly to control towns and land.
2. **Feudal system:** William owned all the land. He gave land to barons, who promised knights and soldiers in return. Barons gave land to knights.
3. **Harrying of the North (1069 to 1070):** William destroyed crops, homes and villages to punish rebels.
4. **Domesday Book (1086):** A survey recording land, people and resources, used to tax and control England.

## 5. Key vocabulary

- **Claimant:** someone who says they have a right to the throne.
- **Witan:** the council of advisers to the Anglo-Saxon king.
- **Baron:** a powerful landowner who held land directly from the king.
- **Knight:** a mounted soldier who served a lord in return for land.
- **Feudal system:** land held in return for service and loyalty.
- **Motte and bailey:** a castle built on a mound, with a walled yard.
- **Harrying:** destroying crops, homes and villages to punish people.
- **Shield wall:** soldiers standing close together with their shields locked.
- **Domesday Book:** a survey of England recorded in 1086.

## 6. Common mistakes

- **Not this:** "William won because he had a bigger army." **But this:** William had archers and cavalry, and Harold's army was tired after marching north and fighting at Stamford Bridge.
- **Not this:** "The Domesday Book was a book of laws." **But this:** It was a survey recording land, people and resources.
- **Not this:** "Barons were the king's servants." **But this:** Barons held land from the king in return for knights and soldiers.
- **Not this:** "Feudal means the king was poor." **But this:** Feudal describes how land was held in return for service.

## 7. Links

- **Builds on:** Anglo-Saxon England and the Witan.
- **Leads to:** Norman control of England and the medieval kingdom.
