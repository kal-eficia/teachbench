# Year 7 Spanish: 10-day retrieval starters

Ten starters, one per lesson for the next two weeks. Each takes about 10 minutes and has six questions. Students answer from memory, with no notes, no dictionary and no AI. Read the questions aloud, or put them on the board. Students write their answers, then you check against the key at the end.

Topics are mixed in every starter: greetings, numbers 1 to 31, school subjects, opinions, and family. Each day revisits something from earlier in the year, so nothing is only seen once.

---

## Day 1

1. Say "good morning" in Spanish.
2. Write the number 12 in Spanish.
3. Translate: "I like history."
4. Translate: "my brother."
5. Complete: Me gusta el arte porque ______.
6. How do you say "maths" in Spanish?

## Day 2

1. What does "¿Cómo estás?" mean in English?
2. Write the number 31 in Spanish.
3. Translate: "I don't like geography."
4. Write the Spanish for "grandmother."
5. Translate: "My sister is funny."
6. Write the number 17 in Spanish.

## Day 3

1. Write "see you later" in Spanish.
2. Write the number 23 in Spanish.
3. Translate: "English is interesting."
4. Write the Spanish for "father."
5. Complete: No me gusta la música porque ______.
6. Translate: "my cousin" (a boy).

## Day 4

6. "Me gusta" literally means "it pleases me." Why do we say "gusta" and not "gustas"?

## Teacher key

Accept any sensible reason in the "porque" questions as long as it uses "es" with a matching adjective (for example "es divertido," "es interesante," "es aburrida" for PE, "es difícil"). Accept "graciosa" as well as "divertida" for "funny."

**Day 1** (greetings, numbers, subjects, opinions, family)
1. Buenos días. 2. doce. 3. Me gusta la historia. 4. mi hermano. 5. es divertido / es interesante (any reason with es). 6. las matemáticas.

**Day 2** (greetings, numbers, opinions, family, subjects)
1. How are you? 2. treinta y uno. 3. No me gusta la geografía. 4. la abuela. 5. Mi hermana es divertida. 6. diecisiete.

**Day 3** (greetings, numbers, subjects, family, opinions)
1. Hasta luego. 2. veintitrés. 3. El inglés es interesante. 4. el padre (or papá). 5. es aburrida (or any reason with es). 6. mi primo.

**Day 4** (greetings, numbers, subjects, family, opinions)
1. Buenas tardes. 2. nueve. 3. Me gusta la educación física. 4. mamá (or madre). 5. es interesante. 6. "Gusta" agrees with the thing liked, which is singular (la historia), so it is "gusta" and not "gustas."

**Day 5** (greetings, numbers, family, opinions, introductions)
1. Buenas noches. 2. catorce. 3. No me gustan las matemáticas. (Plural subject, so "gustan.") 4. el tío. 5. es difícil (or any reason with es). 6. Me llamo Sara.

**Day 6** (numbers, family, opinions, greetings, subjects)
1. veintiocho. 2. el abuelo. 3. Very well. 4. Me gusta el español porque es divertido. 5. el arte. 6. Hola.

**Day 7** (numbers, family, subjects, opinions, farewells)
1. veinticinco. 2. mi mamá (or mi madre). 3. No me gusta la informática. 4. la educación física. 5. es divertido (or any reason with es). 6. Hasta luego.

**Day 8** (opinions, family, numbers, greetings)
1. cinco. 2. mi tía. 3. Me gusta / No me gusta. 4. treinta. 5. Mi padre es amable. 6. Good morning.

**Day 9** (numbers, family, subjects, greetings)
1. dieciséis. 2. mi hijo. 3. Me gusta la ciencia. 4. Hola, ¿cómo estás? 5. once. 6. No, no me gusta el arte.

**Day 10** (review of all topics)
1. veintiuno. 2. No me gusta la educación física porque es aburrida. 3. hermano / hermana. 4. Buenas noches. 5. es interesante (or any reason with es). 6. abuelo; Me llamo.

**Why these starters:** Each day mixes a number, a greeting or farewell, a subject, an opinion, and a family word, so students retrieve several topics at once. Question 6 on Day 4 asks students to think about grammar, not just recall words. Use it to start a short class discussion. Days 1 and 10 cover the whole year, so they make a useful check of what has stuck.
