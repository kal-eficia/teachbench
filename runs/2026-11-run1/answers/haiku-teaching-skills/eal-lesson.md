# Including a new arrival in next week's photosynthesis lesson

**Assumptions:** one-hour lesson, mixed Year 9 class. She has been in the UK for two months, reads Dari (Persian script), and understands some Pashto. She has studied science before, so the idea of photosynthesis is not new to her. Only the English words are. Have every Dari translation checked by a fluent speaker before you print it.

## Before the lesson

- Find out what she covered in science at her last school. Ask through a bilingual colleague or your EAL team if you have one. She may already know the idea and only needs the English words for it.
- Seat her near the front, next to a patient partner who speaks clearly. The partner is a study buddy, not her interpreter for the whole lesson.
- Tell her she can plan, make notes and label diagrams in Dari.

## Key words to pre-teach (7 words)

| Word | Picture idea | Simple meaning | Dari (check first) |
|---|---|---|---|
| leaf | a green flat part of a plant | where the plant makes its food | برگ (barg) |
| light | the sun shining | the energy the plant uses | روشنی (roshni) |
| water | a drop, roots in soil | taken in through the roots | آب (ab) |
| air | arrows around a leaf | carbon dioxide comes from here | هوا (hawa) |
| chlorophyll | green dots inside a leaf cell | catches the light | کلروفیل (kloroflil) |
| glucose | a sugar cube | the food the plant makes | گلوکوز (glukoz) |
| oxygen | bubbles leaving the leaf | released into the air | اکسیجن (oksijen) |

## Simplified instructions

Write each instruction on its own line, with the same verbs every time:

1. Look at the diagram.
2. Label: light, water, air.
3. Talk to your partner. Use the frame below.
4. Write your answer. Use the frame again.

## Sentence frames

- "A plant needs ___ and ___ to make ___."
- "Light energy is changed into ___ energy in the leaf."
- "The plant makes ___, and it releases ___ into the air."
- "This is because ___."
- "Animals ___, but plants ___."

## Visual support

- A labelled leaf cross-section with arrows for light, water, carbon dioxide, glucose and oxygen.
- A word equation with small pictures under each word: sunlight + water + carbon dioxide → glucose + oxygen.

## Lesson flow

1. **5 min:** She matches pictures to the key words. No reading needed.
2. **10 min:** Teach from the diagram, pointing at each arrow as you say it.
3. **5 min:** Pairs use the sentence frames to explain the process to each other.
4. **15 min:** Independent task. She labels the diagram, then writes three lines using the frames.
5. **5 min:** Exit question. She can answer by pointing at the diagram or drawing.

## Keeping it fair and demanding

- Ask the same question you ask everyone, with the same level of challenge. Give her time to think before any cold-calling, and let her answer by pointing or drawing at first.
- Check she understands the question before you mark her answer. Ask her to say the task back in her own words.
- Do not lower the science. If she can explain why a leaf needs light, she has the content, even if her spelling is still developing.

## What to fade first

Fade the translations and the pictures first, once she is matching words confidently. Keep the sentence frames the longest, because they carry the thinking.
