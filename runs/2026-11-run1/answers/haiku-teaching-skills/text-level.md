Rewritten for a Year 7 reader at about reading age 9. All of the geography is kept. I haven't been able to measure the reading age exactly, so the level is estimated from short sentences and everyday words.

===
Tropical rainforests have a huge number of different plants and animals. This is called biodiversity. The weather is hot all year round, and it rains more than 2,000 millimetres every year.

The forest has four layers. The top layer is the emergent layer, where the tallest trees stick up above everything else. Below it is the canopy, a thick roof of leaves. Under the canopy is the understorey, a shady layer of smaller trees and plants. At the bottom is the forest floor, which gets very little light. Each layer has plants and animals that are suited to how much light and damp air it gets.

Deforestation means cutting down forests. Most of it happens because of farming to sell crops, logging (cutting down trees for wood) and building roads and other buildings. Deforestation is a big threat to rainforests. It breaks habitats into small pieces. Some plants and animals are found only in one place (endemic species), so they can disappear for good. Trees also store carbon. When they are cut down and burned or left to rot, that carbon goes into the air. This makes human-caused climate change worse.
===

**Glossary**
- **Biodiversity:** the variety of plants and animals in one place.
- **Emergent layer:** the top layer of the rainforest, made of the tallest trees.
- **Canopy:** the thick layer of leaves below the emergent layer.
- **Understorey:** the shady layer of smaller trees and plants below the canopy.
- **Forest floor:** the bottom layer of the rainforest, which gets very little light.
- **Deforestation:** cutting down forests.
- **Logging:** cutting down trees for wood.
- **Habitat fragmentation:** when a habitat is split into small pieces.
- **Endemic species:** plants or animals that are found in only one place.
- **Carbon:** an element that trees store, which goes into the air when they are burned or rot.

**Comprehension questions (core, for the whole class)**
1. What is biodiversity?
2. Name the four layers of the rainforest, from the top down.
3. Why does the forest floor get very little light?
4. Name two causes of deforestation.
5. Explain why losing rainforest makes climate change worse.

**Extension question:** An animal lives only in the canopy. What might happen to it if the canopy is cut down, and why?
