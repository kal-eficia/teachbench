# Why we have day and night (Year 1)

## Teacher script (about two minutes)

**Hook (what they already know):** "Who has seen the sun in the sky? When does it go away? Where does it go?"

Take two or three answers. Accept them all.

**A concrete example first:** Hold up a torch and a ball. Shine the torch on the ball in a darkened room. "Look. One side of the ball is bright. The other side is dark."

**The idea:** "The Earth is a big ball, like this one. The sun shines on one side of the Earth at a time. The side with the sun is day. The side facing away from the sun is night."

**The movement:** "The Earth is always turning, slowly, like a spinning top. Let's turn the ball slowly. Watch the bright side move." Turn the ball. "As the Earth turns, a part of the world moves into the sunshine, and a part moves into the dark. That is why we have day and night."

**Second example:** "Think about the sun in the morning. When the sun comes up over your part of the world, your part of the world is in day. Where do you think it is night right now, in a different country?"

**Non-example:** "The sun does not go to bed, and it does not go behind the clouds at night. The clouds just block it for a while. At night, the sun is still shining, just on the other side of the Earth."

**Analogy and where it breaks:** "The sun is like a torch that stays still, and the Earth is like a ball that turns." The torch is only the sun in the sense of being a light source. Unlike a real torch, the sun is far, far away and much bigger than the Earth.

## Check question

"If the Earth keeps turning, what happens to the bright side of the ball?"

**Expected answer:** It moves into the dark, so the place that was in day becomes night.

**Most likely wrong answer:** "The sun goes to bed." Use the non-example to correct this, then ask again.

## Read-later version for children

The sun does not go away at night. The Earth is a big ball, and it spins around slowly. When your side of the Earth turns to face the sun, it is day. When your side turns away from the sun, it is night. The sun is still shining on the other side of the world.
