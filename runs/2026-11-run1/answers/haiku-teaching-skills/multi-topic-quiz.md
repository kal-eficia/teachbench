# Year 9 Physics: Mixed Quiz

**Topics:** energy stores and transfers, waves, forces (resultant forces and Newton's first law)

**Instructions:** Choose one answer for each question. Write the letter next to the number.

---

**1. An apple is held high above the ground. Which energy store does it have because of its height?**

- A. Kinetic
- B. Gravitational potential
- C. Elastic potential
- D. Thermal

**2. A kettle heats water. Which energy transfer happens?**

- A. Chemical to light
- B. Thermal to kinetic
- C. Kinetic to electrical
- D. Electrical to thermal

**3. The number of complete waves passing a point each second is called the:**

- A. Amplitude
- B. Wavelength
- C. Frequency
- D. Speed

**4. What is the amplitude of a wave?**

- A. The distance from the rest position to the top of the wave
- B. The distance from one crest to the next
- C. The number of waves produced each second
- D. The speed at which the wave travels

**5. Sound travels as a wave. What type of wave is it?**

- A. Transverse
- B. Electromagnetic
- C. Stationary
- D. Longitudinal

**6. Two forces act on a box. One is 6 N to the right and the other is 4 N to the left. What is the resultant force?**

- A. 10 N to the right
- B. 2 N to the right
- C. 2 N to the left
- D. 0 N

**7. An object has two forces acting on it that are equal in size and opposite in direction. What happens to the object?**

- A. It stays at rest or keeps moving at a steady speed in a straight line
- B. It speeds up
- C. It slows down and stops
- D. It accelerates in the direction of the larger force

**8. Which statement is Newton's first law?**

- A. Force equals mass multiplied by acceleration
- B. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction
- C. An object stays at rest or keeps moving at a steady speed unless a resultant force acts on it
- D. Heavier objects always fall faster than lighter ones

**9. A car brakes suddenly and the passengers lurch forward. Why does this happen?**

- A. The car pushes them forward
- B. Gravity pulls them forward
- C. The seat pushes them forward
- D. Their bodies try to keep moving forward

**10. Which two factors does the kinetic energy of a moving car depend on?**

- A. Mass and colour
- B. Mass and speed
- C. Speed and height
- D. Height and mass

---

ANSWER KEY: 1-B, 2-D, 3-C, 4-A, 5-D, 6-B, 7-A, 8-C, 9-D, 10-B
