# Year 8 maths: three hinge questions on adding fractions with different denominators

Each question is answered by the whole class at the same moment, for example on mini whiteboards or by a show of cards. The position of the correct answer changes across the three options so they do not form a pattern.

---

## Option 1

**What is 1/2 + 1/3?**

- A. 2/5
- B. 5/6
- C. 2/6
- D. 1/6

**Correct answer: B (5/6)**

**What each wrong option reveals**

- **A (2/5):** the student added the tops and the bottoms. They think the denominator adds like the numerator.
- **C (2/6):** the student added the tops, then multiplied the bottoms. They see that a common denominator is needed but do not use it as a shared size.
- **D (1/6):** the student multiplied the tops and the bottoms, as they would when multiplying fractions.

**Decision rule**

- 80% or more choose B: move on to a question with a larger denominator, such as 1/4 + 1/3.
- Many choose A or C: reteach that the denominator names the size of the piece and cannot be added. Use a bar model with thirds and halves split into sixths.
- Many choose D: go back to finding a common denominator. Show that multiplying gives a different question.

---

## Option 2

**What is 2/3 + 1/4?**

- A. 3/7
- B. 3/4
- C. 5/12
- D. 11/12

**Correct answer: D (11/12)**

**What each wrong option reveals**

- **A (3/7):** the student added the tops and the bottoms.
- **B (3/4):** the student added the tops and kept the second denominator. They have not changed the bottom of 2/3 at all.
- **C (5/12):** the student changed the bottoms to 12 but did not change the top of 2/3. The denominator was scaled and the numerator was not.

**Decision rule**

- 80% or more choose D: move on to subtraction or mixed numbers.
- Many choose B: reteach the step of changing both fractions to the common denominator, with each fraction shown separately.
- Many choose C: show that whatever is done to the bottom must also be done to the top. Use a pair of equivalent fractions, such as 2/3 = 8/12, to check.

---

## Option 3

**What is 1/4 + 2/5?**

- A. 13/20
- B. 1/3
- C. 3/20
- D. 8/20

**Correct answer: A (13/20)**

**What each wrong option reveals**

- **B (1/3):** the student added the tops and the bottoms, giving 3/9.
- **C (3/20):** the student added the tops and multiplied the bottoms. This is the same error as option 1, option C.
- **D (8/20):** the student changed 2/5 into 8/20 and then left out the first fraction.

**Decision rule**

- 80% or more choose A: the class is ready for a mixed question, so move on.
- Many choose B or C: go back to the common denominator idea. Ask the class to say what 4 and 5 both go into before they add anything.
- Many choose D: check that the first fraction was converted too, then ask the class to write both converted fractions on their whiteboards before adding.
