# Year 7 Form Time: First Term, Four Weeks

Fifteen minutes each morning. Organisation, friendships and phones run through the four weeks, with a short reflection each Friday so students can see what's working. Check your school's phone policy before Week 3 and use the school's wording where it differs from this plan.

## The four-week overview

- **Week 1: Getting organised.** Planners, the week's deadlines, one study habit.
- **Week 2: Friendships.** What good friendships look like, and how to handle disagreements.
- **Week 3: Phones.** Habits, notifications, and when to put the phone away.
- **Week 4: Bringing it together.** Revisit each theme, set a personal target, and reflect.

## Full plan: Weeks 1 and 2

### Week 1: Getting organised

**Monday: Planner check (15 min)**
- Aim: students know how to use their planner and have every deadline for the week written in.
- Activity: students open their planner and write in each lesson's homework and any deadline the teacher reads out. Pair checks with a neighbour.
- Prompts: "What's the first thing you'll do when you get home today?"

**Tuesday: Reading (15 min)**
- Aim: a short, shared reading to start a conversation.
- Activity: read a short extract about a young person organising their time. Students answer two questions in pairs.
- Prompts: "What did the character do that helped?" "What's one thing you could copy this week?"

**Wednesday: Current events (15 min)**
- Aim: a neutral look at one news story that students can talk about safely.
- Activity: explain one story neutrally (choose something age-appropriate, such as a school or sport story). Ask for opinions without expecting anyone to be right or wrong.
- Prompts: "What do you think, and why?" "What's one fact you'd want to check first?"

**Thursday: Futures (15 min)**
- Aim: connect school to the skills people use at work.
- Activity: name three skills employers value (such as teamwork, time management, communication). Students write one example of each from their week.
- Prompts: "Which of these do you already do well?"

**Friday: Reflection (15 min)**
- Aim: notice wins and set one small improvement.
- Activity: each student writes one win and one thing to improve next week on a sticky note. Put them on the board or in a box. Nobody reads them aloud unless they want to.
- Prompts: "What went well this week?" "What's one thing you'll change?"

### Week 2: Friendships

**Monday: What makes a good friend (15 min)**
- Aim: students think about the qualities they value, without anyone being singled out.
- Activity: students write three qualities of a good friend on a card, anonymously. Shuffle and display the most common ideas.
- Prompts: "Which of these would you want a friend to have?"

**Tuesday: Reading (15 min)**
- Aim: a short story about a disagreement between friends.
- Activity: read a short extract. Pairs discuss what each character could have said differently.
- Prompts: "What's one thing you could say instead of walking away?" "What would help someone feel included?"

**Wednesday: Current events (15 min)**
- Aim: talk about how people treat each other online and offline.
- Activity: one neutral example (for example, a news story about a community project). Students say what kindness looks like in practice.
- Prompts: "What do you think, and why?" "What's one small kind thing you could do this week?"

**Thursday: Futures (15 min)**
- Aim: link friendships to teamwork at work.
- Activity: students name a job where working well with others matters. Discuss one thing that helps a team succeed.
- Prompts: "What would you do if a team member was struggling?"

**Friday: Reflection (15 min)**
- Aim: notice wins and set one improvement.
- Activity: the same sticky-note routine as Week 1. Add a line: "One person I'd like to get to know better."
- Prompts: "Who did you talk to this week that you hadn't before?"

## Weeks 3 and 4: one line per day

**Week 3 (phones)**
- Mon: planner check, and one phone habit to try this week (for example, phone in bag during form time).
- Tue: a short article on screen time, two discussion questions in pairs.
- Wed: a neutral news story about online safety, "what do you think and why?"
- Thu: careers link: which jobs use phones for good, and what good phone habits look like at work.
- Fri: reflection sticky notes, plus one phone habit they've kept.

**Week 4 (bringing it together)**
- Mon: planner check with a new deadline set by each student for the following week.
- Tue: a short reading on study habits, two questions on what they'd keep.
- Wed: a current event with a positive angle, one thing students can do in their own community.
- Thu: a "skills I've built" list, each student names one skill from the term.
- Fri: final reflection: each student writes a personal target for the next term and reads it to themselves, not aloud.

## Resources to print or display

- Planner-check checklist (one line per deadline and one study habit)
- Two short reading extracts per week (one for Tuesday, one for Thursday if needed)
- Sticky notes and a box for Friday reflections
- A simple board with the four themes listed, so students know what each day is about
- A short signpost card for any student who needs to talk to someone, showing who they can go to in school
