# Six-week review: [S1] (Year 11 GCSE Maths)

**Assumptions:** The baseline and week-6 tests used the same style, so the two can be compared topic by topic. Each topic is based on a small number of questions, so treat the percentages as indicative rather than precise. Statistics was not in the baseline, so no change can be measured for it.

On the five topics tested at both points, the average rose from 43% to 59%.

**Habits:** attended 11 of 12 sessions (92%). Homework completed 4 of 6 times (67%).

---

## Analysis for the tutor

**What has moved.** Algebra (+35) and geometry (+25) show the biggest gains, and number has also improved (+15). Whatever you did in these areas is working, so keep the same approach there.

**What has barely moved.** Ratio and proportion rose only 5 points. [S1] says it feels confusing and has been avoiding it, so avoidance is probably part of the problem, not just a knowledge gap. The next step is to find the specific misconception. Common causes at this level include treating ratio as additive rather than multiplicative (for example, thinking that if 3 becomes 6, then 5 becomes 8), and not using a unit or bar model to work out the scale.

**What has not moved.** Probability stayed at 50%. Check whether it was taught but not retrieved in sessions, or whether a particular idea (such as combined events, tree diagrams or sample space) is the sticking point.

**Habits.** Attendance is strong. Homework is the weaker habit, and the homework gap may be connected to the ratio avoidance, since the tasks [S1] is least confident with are the ones most likely to be left undone.

**The question the parents are asking.** Yes, tutoring is working in algebra, geometry and number. It is not yet working in ratio and probability. Those two need a different plan rather than more of the same.

## Three priorities for the next six weeks

1. **Ratio and proportion (most important).** Run a five-question diagnostic in week 7 to find the exact misconception. Use the unit method and bar models, and start each of two weekly sessions with one short ratio question that [S1] can get right. Aim for at least 60% on the same style of test by week 12.
2. **Probability.** Spend two sessions on tree diagrams and sample space, with a five-minute retrieval starter at the beginning of each one. Aim for 65% or more.
3. **Homework routine.** Agree two short homework tasks a week (about 15 minutes each), with one of them on ratio, and check them at the start of each session. Aim for 6 of 6 completed.
