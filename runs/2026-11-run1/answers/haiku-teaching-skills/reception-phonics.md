# Phonics Session: Introducing the Digraph 'sh' (20 minutes, Reception, 26 children)

## Assumptions

- The class follows a systematic synthetic phonics programme (Letters and Sounds style, Phase 3 and 4).
- Children already know the letters s, a, t, p, i, n, m, d, g, o, c, k, e, u, r, h, b, f, l and the digraph ck. Letters not yet taught are j, v, w, x, y, z and q, so the words below use only known graphemes.
- Each child has a mini whiteboard and pen.

## Learning focus

Children recognise the digraph sh as one sound /sh/, read it in words, and write it.

## Session plan

**1. Revisit (3 minutes).** Flash the known graphemes, including ck. Children say each sound, then blend three quick words: sack, pick, mud.

**2. Introduce sh (4 minutes).**
- Show the card "sh". Say: "Two letters, one sound: shhh." Children copy the sound with a finger to their lips.
- Model writing sh on the board, starting at the top of the s and joining into the h. Children write it on their whiteboards.
- Explain that it's a digraph: two letters making one sound.

**3. Blend and read (5 minutes).** Use the teacher's card set. Start with words at the start of the word, then the end:
- Start: ship, shop, shed, shut, shin
- End: fish, dish, wish, rash, mash

Model with "sh... i... p, ship". Children read each word with the teacher, then alone in chorus. Point out that sh comes first in ship and last in fish.

**4. Listen and point (3 minutes).** Say a list of words, some with sh and some without (sip, ship, pin, shell, net). Children show a thumbs up if they hear sh, and a thumbs down if they don't. Keep it quick and bright.

**5. Read a short sentence (3 minutes).** Put "Fish in a dish." on the board, then "Sid has a shed." Children read each sentence with the teacher, then one at a time, with praise for every attempt.

**6. Write (2 minutes).** Children write sh on their whiteboards, then one sh word they know or can sound out. Hold up whiteboards and say the word together.

## Teacher tips

- Use the same sound action every time (finger to lips) and keep to one new sound per session.
- Watch for children who write s and h as two separate letters with a gap. Reinforce the joined sh.
- Support: give children a picture card for each word, with the sound card underneath as a reminder.
- Stretch: children who finish early sound out ship, shop and shed on their whiteboards and write a sentence with one sh word.

## Exit check (end of session)

Hold up a card and ask each child to read it aloud, one at a time or in a group, depending on how long you have. Use cards: shop, wish, fish. Note who needs another go at sh in tomorrow's session.
