# Revision timetable: Year 11 student (three weeks to mocks)

**Assumptions:** the student is weakest in algebra and ratio (Maths) and in remembering dates and events (History). Mocks: Maths Paper 1 on Monday of week 4, English Language on Tuesday, Biology on Wednesday, History on Thursday. School ends at 3:15. Football is Tuesday and Thursday, 5:00 to 6:30. Mosque class is Saturday, 10:00 to 12:00. Friday is a lighter day, and the student takes a midday break for Jumu'ah. The student can focus for about 30 minutes, so every block is 30 minutes with a 5-minute break.

## The principles behind this plan

- **Short blocks.** Each block is 30 minutes: 5 minutes of recall from memory, 20 minutes of practice, 5 minutes to check.
- **Active methods only.** Practice questions, flashcards, and explaining a topic out loud. Reading notes on their own does not count.
- **Spaced and mixed.** Each topic comes back after two or three days, then after a week. Subjects are mixed within the day.
- **Buffer.** About 10% of slots are left empty for catch-up.

## How to do one revision block (30 minutes)

1. **5 minutes:** Close the notes. Write down everything you remember about the topic.
2. **20 minutes:** Do practice questions or make and test flashcards. Check answers as you go.
3. **5 minutes:** Note the two things you got wrong and what you will do with them next time.

## Weekly check-in (Sunday, 10 minutes)

- What did I finish this week?
- What do I need to move to next week?
- Rate each topic from 1 to 5: algebra, ratio, dates, English, Biology.
- Which topic will get an extra block next week?

## Notes for the student and family

- Week 3 is the most important week for timed past questions.
- In week 4, the aim is to rest well and keep the routine. Do not start new topics.
