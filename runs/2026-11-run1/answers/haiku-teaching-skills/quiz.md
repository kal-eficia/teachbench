# Year 7 Geography: Rivers Check

Name: ______________________    Class: ________    Date: ________

This is a low-stakes check. It's here to help you remember what you've learned, so have a go at every question, even if you're not sure.

For each question, circle one letter. Then tick how sure you are.

Confidence key: **G** = guessing, **T** = think so, **C** = certain

---

**1. What is the source of a river?**

A. The place where the river flows into the sea
B. The place where two rivers join together
C. The place where the river begins, often on high ground
D. The place where the river flows into a lake

Confidence: G / T / C

**2. Which process is the wearing away of the river bed and banks by material the river carries?**

A. Abrasion
B. Attrition
C. Solution
D. Deposition

Confidence: G / T / C

**3. Which method of transport moves larger stones by rolling them along the river bed?**

A. Suspension
B. Solution
C. Saltation
D. Traction

Confidence: G / T / C

**4. Where is deposition most likely to happen along a river?**

A. On the outside of a bend, where the water is fastest
B. On the inside of a bend, where the water is slow
C. In the steep upper course, near the source
D. In fast rapids where the water is rushing

Confidence: G / T / C

**5. What is a meander?**

A. A bend in a river that gets bigger over time
B. A straight stretch of river with no bends
C. A waterfall formed by hard rock
D. A lake formed where a river meets the sea

Confidence: G / T / C

**6. How does a waterfall form?**

A. The river flows over soft rock only
B. The river flows across a flat plain
C. Hard rock sits above softer rock, and the softer rock erodes faster underneath
D. The river meets the sea

Confidence: G / T / C

**7. Which of these increases the risk of flooding in a river catchment?**

A. More trees growing along the banks
B. Permeable soil that lets water soak in
C. Slow snowmelt in spring
D. Heavy rain falling on concrete surfaces in towns

Confidence: G / T / C

**8. What is a levee?**

A. A channel dug to speed up the flow of water
B. A raised bank built along a river to stop it overflowing
C. A sandy beach where a river meets the sea
D. A sudden drop in the river bed

Confidence: G / T / C

**9. Which landform forms at a river mouth through deposition?**

A. A waterfall
B. An interlocking spur
C. A meander
D. A delta

Confidence: G / T / C

**10. Which landform forms in the upper course, where a river winds around hills?**

A. Interlocking spurs
B. An oxbow lake
C. A floodplain
D. An estuary

Confidence: G / T / C

**11. Which of these is a valid four-figure grid reference?**

A. North-east
B. 5 km
C. 4523
D. 1:50 000

Confidence: G / T / C

**12. What does a scale of 1:50 000 mean?**

A. 1 cm on the map is 50 m on the ground
B. 1 cm on the map is 500 m on the ground
C. 1 cm on the map is 50 km on the ground
D. 1 cm on the map is 5 km on the ground

Confidence: G / T / C

## For the teacher (not for students)

Questions 1 to 10 come from the rivers unit taught two weeks ago. Questions 11 and 12 are older map skills, included so students retrieve something from further back.

| Q | Topic | Why the wrong options tempt students |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Source | A is the mouth; B is a confluence; D mixes up source and mouth |
| 2 | Erosion | B (attrition) is rock-on-rock wear, not wear on the bed; C is dissolving; D is deposition |
| 3 | Transport | C (saltation) is bouncing, not rolling; A and B are suspension and solution |
| 4 | Deposition | A is where erosion happens; C and D are erosion-heavy areas |
| 5 | Meander | B is a straight channel; C is a waterfall; D is an estuary or delta |
| 6 | Waterfall | A and B leave out the hard-over-soft layering; D is the mouth |
| 7 | Flooding | A and B increase infiltration or slow runoff; C is slow melt, not a flood trigger |
| 8 | Levee | A is a channel; C is a beach; D is a rapid drop in the bed |
| 9 | Landforms at the mouth | A is an upper-course feature; B and C are upper or middle features |
| 10 | Upper course | B is a cut-off lower-course feature; C and D are lower-course features |
| 11 | Grid reference | A is a compass direction; B and D are a distance and a scale |
| 12 | Scale | Students often confuse 1:50 000 with 1 cm = 50 m (A) or 50 km (C) |

**What to do with the results:** note the two most-missed questions and put them in next lesson's starter. Students who are "certain" and wrong are the most useful to talk through.
