# Year 8 French: Homework

**Time:** about 25 minutes.

**Who does what:** your child does this alone. You don't need to know any French to help. If you'd like to be involved, ask them to show you the sentence they're proudest of.

## Part 1: The perfect tense with avoir (about 10 minutes)

Write the past participle in each gap, so the sentence is in the perfect tense with *avoir*. Some verbs are in the infinitive in brackets to help you.

1. Hier, j'ai ____________ (jouer) au foot avec mon frère.
2. Ce matin, nous avons ____________ (manger) des crêpes.
3. Le week-end dernier, tu as ____________ (finir) ton devoir.
4. Samedi, mes parents ont ____________ (acheter) un nouveau canapé.
5. Mon ami a ____________ (faire) ses devoirs dans le bus.
6. L'année dernière, j'ai ____________ (lire) un livre très long.
7. Ma sœur a ____________ (voir) un film au cinéma.
8. Hier, nous avons ____________ (écrire) une lettre à grand-mère.

## Part 2: Family words (about 5 minutes)

Write the French for each English phrase, with the correct *mon*, *ma* or *mes*.

1. my brother
2. my little sister
3. my grandmother
4. my uncle

## Part 3: Opinions (about 10 minutes)

Write three sentences in French. Each one should give an opinion with *j'aime* or *je déteste*, and then give a reason with *parce que*.

Example: *J'aime le football parce que c'est rapide.*

Use a different sport, food or subject in each sentence.

## Check before you finish

Read each sentence out loud in your head. Does each perfect tense use *avoir* plus the past participle? Does each opinion have a reason after *parce que*? Fix anything that doesn't fit, then hand it in.
