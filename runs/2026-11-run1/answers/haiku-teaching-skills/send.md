# Adapted source task: Charles I and the English Civil War (Year 8, 30 minutes)

**Assumptions:** the three sources on pages 42 to 44 stay exactly as they are. The question stays the same: "Charles I was mainly to blame for the outbreak of the Civil War." How far do you agree? The judgement is still open. The changes are to the route, not the destination. You can adapt the sheet to the printed layout, and the sources can be given as a text file or read aloud if the student prefers.

## Before the lesson (for you)

- Print the sheet on cream or pale paper in a sans-serif font at 14 pt with 1.5 spacing. Keep each part on its own half-page.
- Put the timings on the board and on the sheet, with a visual timetable at the top: Read, Plan, Write, Judgement.
- Tell the autistic student the structure and any change in advance, and tell them which part of the task is fixed.
- Check the student's support plan or SENCO advice for agreed adjustments, and follow those first.

## Adapted sheet (for the student)

**Task:** Read the three sources on pages 42 to 44. Then answer this question: *"Charles I was mainly to blame for the outbreak of the Civil War." How far do you agree?*

You will use about 30 minutes in four parts.

### Part 1: Read the sources (6 minutes)

Use the grid. Write a few words in each box. Short notes are fine.

| | Who wrote it? | What does it say caused the war? | Does it blame Charles? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source A (page 42) | | | |
| Source B (page 43) | | | |
| Source C (page 44) | | | |

*Key words to know (in bold in the sources): Personal Rule, Ship Money, Petition of Right, Long Parliament.*

### Part 2: Plan your answer (5 minutes)

Choose one way to answer, and tick it:

- [ ] Charles was mainly to blame, because ____________
- [ ] Charles was partly to blame, but ____________ also mattered
- [ ] Other causes were more important, because ____________

Then fill in the plan for three paragraphs:

**Paragraph 1 (P, E, E, L):** Point: ____________ Evidence from a source: ____________ Explain: ____________ Link back to the question: ____________

**Paragraph 2 (other causes):** Point: ____________ Evidence: ____________ Explain: ____________ Link: ____________

**Paragraph 3 (judgement):** Point: ____________ Evidence: ____________ Explain: ____________ Link: ____________

### Part 3: Write (15 minutes)

Write your answer in the boxes below, one paragraph at a time. You can start each paragraph with one of these sentence starters:

- Paragraph 1: "One reason Charles was to blame is..."
- Paragraph 2: "However, another cause was..."
- Paragraph 3: "Overall, I agree that..."

You can write it, type it, or say it into a recording and ask your teacher to type it up. Pause after each paragraph and tick it off.

### Part 4: Judgement (4 minutes)

Finish with one judgement sentence. Choose one of these starters, then finish it in your own words:

- "On balance, Charles I was mainly to blame because..."
- "Charles I was partly to blame, but..."
- "Other causes mattered more, because..."

## What changed and why

| Barrier | Change |
|---|---|
| Reading (dyslexia) | Sans-serif font, 14 pt, 1.5 spacing, key words in bold and defined in a box, sources available as audio, short numbered steps. |
| Attention (ADHD) | Chunked into four timed parts with a visible checklist. A tick box at the end of each paragraph. Permission to stand or stretch at the end of Part 2. |
| Open-ended anxiety (autism) | Visual timetable and a warning before each part. Fixed structure (the plan and the paragraph frame), with the open judgement kept. Sentence starters and a choice of three judgement starters, so the open question feels less empty. Option to work alone. |
| Writing load | Typed, spoken or written answers are all accepted. |

The question, the sources, the marking points and the expectation of a judgement are unchanged, so every student still works at the same level.

## When to fade each support

- **Sentence starters:** remove after the student has written three PEEL paragraphs without them, usually by the next extended task.
- **Plan frame (Part 2):** remove after two tasks where the student plans without it. Keep the plan as a blank page.
- **Judgement starters:** remove once the student writes a judgement in their own words on two tasks.
- **Timed chunks and visual timetable:** keep for the autistic student while they are settling into the new routine, then fade to the class timings.
- **Audio sources:** keep as an option. Remove only if the student asks.
