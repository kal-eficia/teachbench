# Year 10 end-of-topic results: term summary

**Assumption:** Bioenergetics covers photosynthesis and respiration. The baseline was given before the term and the end-of-topic tests came at the end of each topic. These are different papers, so treat the change figures as a rough guide.

## Comparison (class averages)

| Topic | Baseline (before term) | End-of-topic test | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cells | 38% | 72% | +34 points |
| Organisation | 40% | 58% | +18 points |
| Infection and response | 35% | 65% | +30 points |
| Bioenergetics | 30% | 41% | +11 points |

## Summary for the head of department

Every topic has moved up this term. Cells made the biggest gain and is now at 72%. Infection and response rose 30 points to 65%. Organisation is at 58%, which is a solid gain on a 40% start, but it needs more practice to hold. Bioenergetics is the priority. At 41% it is still below half the class, and it gained only 11 points.

## What I will do next

1. **Bioenergetics: reteach the core.** I will give a 10-question check on photosynthesis and respiration first, to see whether the problem is the equations, the processes or the vocabulary. I will then reteach the weakest part with worked examples and run two short retrieval starters a week. Target: 55% or higher at the next test.
2. **Organisation: regular retrieval.** A five-question starter each week on organ systems and transport, so the material is recalled rather than only read. Target: 65% or higher.
3. **Keep Cells and Infection going.** One mixed starter a week that revisits both topics, so the gains stick.

I will also look at class attendance and homework completion alongside these results before the next review, to see whether any habit is holding the class back.
