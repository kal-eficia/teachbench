# [School name]: Student Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

**Applies to:** all students, Years 7 to 13 (ages 11 to 18).
**Owner:** [name or role]. **Review date:** [date, one year from adoption].

## Why we have this policy

We want students to use AI to learn better, not to skip the learning. We also want every student to use it safely. This policy sets out what is allowed, what is not, and how to be safe.

## Using AI to learn (allowed)

- Ask AI to explain a topic in a different way, or to give you a simpler version to start from.
- Ask it to quiz you, test your understanding, or give you feedback on a draft you wrote yourself.
- Use it to plan revision, break down a task, or check your reasoning.
- Use it to translate or explain words, if your teacher agrees.

## Not allowed

- Submitting AI-written work as your own, in full or in part, unless a teacher has said the task allows it.
- Using AI during a test, exam or any task marked as "no AI".
- Using AI to write answers you then copy without understanding them.

## Being open about it

If AI helped with a piece of work, say so. Name the tool and say what it did, for example "I used it to check my plan". Your teacher will tell you how to record this for each subject.

## Staying safe

- **Do not share personal information** with any AI tool. That includes your full name, address, school, phone number, photos, and anything about other people.
- **Use school-approved tools** where your school provides them. Check the age rules before using any other tool, because many require users to be at least 13 or have a parent's permission.
- **Check what it says.** AI can make mistakes and can sound confident when it is wrong. Check facts with your textbook, notes or a trusted website.
- **Report it** if an AI tool gives you something harmful, upsetting or inappropriate, or if someone asks you to use AI in a way that worries you. Tell a teacher or a trusted adult straight away.

## Teachers and staff

Teachers will tell students which tasks allow AI and how it should be shown. Where AI is used to check or mark work, the teacher makes the final decision.

## If this policy is broken

Breaking this policy will be handled under the school's behaviour and academic integrity procedures. Your teacher will talk to you first, and the response will depend on what happened and whether it was a first time.
