# Paper 1 Question 4 intervention: six Year 11 students, six weeks

I'm assuming an AQA English Language Paper 1 (Q4 is a 20-mark evaluation of a statement about a source). If your board differs, the structure still works; swap the wording.

## Who and why

- **Group:** the six students below target on Q4 specifically, not on the whole paper. Group by the same gap, not by grade.
- **The gap (to confirm with a quick check):** most students below target on Q4 either give a personal opinion without quoting, or write a balanced summary and never reach a judgement. Use the pre-check to see which applies to each student and adjust Session 2 if most of the group sit in one camp.

## Measure

- **Pre-check (before Session 1):** one Q4 answer, timed at 15 minutes, on a source extract you haven't used with them before.
- **Post-check (Session 6):** a different extract, same timing and same question type, so the score reflects learning and not memory of the text.
- Score both against the 20-mark scheme, using the level descriptors.

## Session plan (30 minutes each, same time every week)

Each session runs the same way so the group can focus on the content: 5 minutes retrieval, 10 minutes teaching with a worked model, 10 minutes practice, 5 minutes exit check.

| Week | Focus | Teaching and practice | Exit check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What a judgement is | Model a Q4 paragraph that ends in a clear view ("This makes the reader feel..., which suggests the writer wants..."). Students rewrite one of their own pre-check paragraphs with a judgement at the end. | Write one sentence that gives a clear judgement on a given statement |
| 2 | Choosing and using evidence | Teach selecting one short quotation per point and zooming in on one word or technique. Practise picking the quote, then analysing it in two sentences. | Pick a quote for a statement and explain its effect in one sentence |
| 3 | "To what extent" and balance | Show how to write one point for the statement and one against, then reach a judgement that weighs them. Students write a paragraph that includes a counter-view. | Give a judgement that names one thing that weakens the statement |
| 4 | Planning and time | Teach a 2-minute plan: statement, three points in order, judgement. Practise planning before writing on a timer. | Plan a full answer in 2 minutes |
| 5 | Full answer under time | Students write a full Q4 under timed conditions (15 minutes). They then check their answer against the level descriptors and highlight one sentence to improve. | Rewrite the highlighted sentence to be sharper |
| 6 | Post-check and review | Post-check (15 minutes), then 10 minutes of feedback. Each student sets one target for their Paper 1 practice. | Post-check score |

## Exit criteria

- A student exits when their post-check reaches their target band (or 14/20 if no target is set).
- Students who haven't reached it continue into a seventh session or are regrouped around the specific gap that remains (judgement, evidence or balance).

## Timing and communication

- Keep the same weekday and time so students don't miss the same lesson every week. If a session clashes with an activity, move it that week and tell students in advance.
- A short message to each family: "Your child is joining a six-week Paper 1 Question 4 group after school on [day]. We're working on giving a clear judgement and using evidence well. Please make sure they can get home safely afterwards."

## Summary for line manager

Six Year 11 students below target on English Language Paper 1 Question 4 will attend six 30-minute after-school sessions over six weeks. The group is measured by a timed pre-check and post-check on the same question type with different source extracts. Sessions target judgement, evidence selection, balance and timed planning. Students exit when they reach their target band on the post-check, and any who don't continue or are regrouped around the gap that remains.
