# 50-minute session: how to revise effectively (Year 10)

**Assumptions:** Year 10 (about 14-15 years old), a classroom with a phone box or basket at the door, and no subject-specific content, so it works for any subject. Students have pens and paper. Devices are optional and used for one demonstration only.

## Session plan

| Time | Activity | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | **Quick start: the blank page** | Students write everything they remember about last lesson's topic in two minutes, with no notes. |
| 4-6 | **Look at the gap** | Students compare what they wrote with their notes. Ask: "How much did you remember without looking?" |
| 6-14 | **Teach: what works and what doesn't** | Explain that re-reading and highlighting feel productive but leave little behind. Testing yourself (retrieval practice) is much stronger. Use the "feeling of knowing" idea: a page can look familiar and still not be remembered. |
| 14-26 | **Practise: brain dump and check** | Students pick one topic from any subject. Close notes, write everything for three minutes, then open notes and check, adding missing points in a different colour. Then they write three questions and swap with a partner to answer. |
| 26-31 | **Space and mix** | Show a simple seven-day chart. Students pick three topics and schedule a 10-minute review of each across the week, mixing them up rather than doing one topic in a block. |
| 31-38 | **Phones out of the room** | Phones go in the box for the whole session. Set a 25-minute focus timer. Ask: "What distracts you most?" Agree a rule for the week: the phone is out of reach for each study block, not face down on the desk. |
| 38-44 | **AI as a quiz master, not an answer machine** | Demonstrate a prompt: "Quiz me on [topic], one question at a time. Don't give the answer until I've tried." Students try the same idea from memory with a partner, then compare it with what the AI would do. |
| 44-50 | **Plan the two-week challenge** | Students complete the tracker below and agree three 25-minute retrieval sessions a week. |

## Student handout: two-week challenge

**Three rules:**
1. Close the book before you revise. Try to recall first, then check.
2. Phone out of reach, in a different room if you can.
3. Use a 25-minute timer, then take a short break.

**Your sessions:** three a week, each 25 minutes.
