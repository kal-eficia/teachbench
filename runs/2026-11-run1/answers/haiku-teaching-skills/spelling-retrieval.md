# Year 2: five mornings on 'kn', 'gn', 'ge' and 'dge'

**Format:** 10 minutes each morning. Every session starts with recall from memory, then a short active task, then a quick check. Students do not copy words out.

**Spelling rules to keep in mind:**
- 'kn' and 'gn' at the start of a word: the 'k' and 'g' are silent.
- 'dge' is used after a single short vowel sound at the end of a word (badge, edge, bridge).
- 'ge' is used for the /j/ sound at the end of a word after a long vowel sound or a consonant (age, huge, change).

## Morning 1: 'kn' and 'gn' words (knock, know, gnaw, gnat)

- **2 min, recall:** Say each word aloud. Students say what the silent letter is.
- **6 min, write from memory:** Say a word. Students write it on a mini whiteboard without looking. Show the word. They tick if correct and try again if not.
- **2 min, check:** Teacher shows the four words. Students compare and fix.

## Morning 2: 'dge' words (badge, edge, bridge, judge, fudge)

- **2 min, recall:** Yesterday's words. Say knock, gnat. Students spell them on a whiteboard.
- **6 min, rule hunt:** Show a short word with a short vowel (e.g. "edge") and a word with a long vowel (e.g. "huge"). Students say which way the /j/ sound is spelt and why.
- **2 min, write:** Say five 'dge' words. Students write them from memory.

## Morning 3: 'ge' words (age, huge, change, cage, page)

- **2 min, recall:** Say three 'dge' words and three 'kn' words. Students write them.
- **6 min, mixed sort:** Students sort cards into "kn or gn," "dge" and "ge." Use a different order for each group so no pattern appears in where the cards start.
- **2 min, check:** Teacher reads the words aloud. Students hold up their sorting.

## Morning 4: Mixed recall

- **2 min, recall:** Say a word, students spell it with the right letters.
- **6 min, fill the gap:** Read short sentences with a gap, such as "The ___ was on the bike." (badge). Students write the missing word from memory.
- **2 min, check:** Read the sentences again with the answers.

## Morning 5: Check

- **2 min, recall:** Quick oral round: "How do you spell the /j/ sound after a short vowel?"
- **6 min, dictation:** Read 8 words and 2 short sentences. Students write them from memory.
- **2 min, teacher check:** Mark the dictation. Note which words each child missed.

## Teacher check

- Mark the Morning 5 dictation quickly, and write a short list of who needs the 'dge' or 'ge' rule again.
- Re-run the Morning 2 rule hunt for any child who still mixes up 'dge' and 'ge' next week.
