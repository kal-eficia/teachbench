# Year 8 Science Cover Pack: Digestive System Review

**Period 3 · 50 minutes · 30 students · Room has textbooks, no computers**

**Assumption:** the class has done the organs and their jobs, and enzymes will be taught by the class teacher on their return. This pack keeps to review and practice, so no new content needs explaining.

---

## Part A: Cover teacher sheet (one page)

**Before the lesson:** Copy the student sheet (the next section) for all 30 students. If you cannot copy it, write the Part 1 questions on the board.

**What to say at the start, word for word:**

1. "Good morning. Today is a review of the digestive system. You'll work on your own from the sheet."
2. "Write your name on the sheet, then start with Part 1. Use your textbook if you get stuck."
3. "When you have finished, put your sheet on the desk. Please don't swap answers."

**Timings:**

| Time | What students do |
|---|---|
| 0 to 10 min | Part 1, quick recall |
| 10 to 35 min | Parts 2 to 4, practice |
| 35 to 42 min | Part 5, short answer |
| 42 to 45 min | Challenge, for early finishers |
| 45 to 50 min | Exit question, then collect |

**Answers:** given in the teacher key below. You don't need to teach anything. If a student asks a question you can't answer, tell them to write it on the back of the sheet for their teacher.

**Collect:** all sheets, with names on. Count them (there should be 30) and leave them in the tray on the teacher's desk.

**If something goes wrong:** no technology is needed. If the sheet cannot be copied, use the board.

---

## Part B: Student sheet

**Digestive System Review**

**Part 1: Quick recall** (short answers)

1. What does digestion mean?
2. What is the name of the tube that carries food from your mouth to your stomach?
3. Which organ absorbs most of the water from undigested food?
4. Why does your body need to digest food?
5. What are the two ends of the digestive system?

**Part 2: Multiple choice** (circle one letter)

6. Where is most digested food absorbed into the blood?
   - A. Large intestine
   - B. Stomach
   - C. Small intestine
   - D. Mouth

7. Which organ's main job is to absorb water from undigested food?
   - A. Large intestine
   - B. Small intestine
   - C. Oesophagus
   - D. Mouth

8. Which organ's job is to carry food from the mouth to the stomach?
   - A. Large intestine
   - B. Stomach
   - C. Small intestine
   - D. Oesophagus

9. What is the main job of your stomach?
   - A. Absorbing water
   - B. Churning food and mixing it with digestive juices
   - C. Carrying food to the mouth
   - D. Making bile

10. Which part of the digestive system stores solid waste before it leaves the body?
    - A. Rectum
    - B. Oesophagus
    - C. Mouth
    - D. Small intestine

11. Where does digestion start?
    - A. Stomach
    - B. Kidney
    - C. Mouth
    - D. Large intestine

**Part 3: Match each organ to its job**

Write the letter of the matching job next to each organ. Each job is used once.

**Part 4: Put the order**

Write the numbers 1 to 5 next to these organs to show the order food travels through them, starting with the first.

____ Small intestine

**Part 5: Short answer**

Food has to be broken into small pieces before it can pass into your blood. Explain why. Write two sentences.

**Challenge (early finishers)**

Draw the digestive system from memory and label every organ you can. Then check your drawing against the diagram in your textbook. Add any organs you missed, in a different colour.

**Exit question** (write this before you hand in your sheet)

Write one question you have about how food is broken down. Your teacher will read it.

## Part C: Teacher key (for the cover teacher)

**Part 1**
1. Breaking food down into smaller substances the body can use.
2. The oesophagus.
3. The large intestine.
4. So that the nutrients can pass into the blood and reach body cells.
5. The mouth and the anus.

**Part 2** (answers in order: C, A, D, B, A, C)
6. C, Small intestine
7. A, Large intestine
8. D, Oesophagus
9. B, Churning food and mixing it with digestive juices
10. A, Rectum
11. C, Mouth

**Part 3**
Mouth: C. Oesophagus: E. Stomach: A. Small intestine: D. Large intestine: B.

**Part 4**
1 Mouth, 2 Oesophagus, 3 Stomach, 4 Small intestine, 5 Large intestine.

**Part 5**
Food has to be small enough to pass through the wall of the small intestine into the blood. Larger pieces cannot get through.

**Challenge**
Students should include the mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine and rectum, with the liver shown as an extra organ if they add it.

**Exit question**
Any sensible question counts. Keep them for the class teacher, who will use them to start the enzymes lesson.
