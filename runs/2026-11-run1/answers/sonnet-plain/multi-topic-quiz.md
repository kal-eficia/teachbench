# Year 9 Physics Quiz: Energy, Waves and Forces

*Assumptions: 10 questions, one mark each, four options per question. Topics are mixed deliberately: energy stores and transfers, waves, and forces (resultant forces and Newton's first law).*

**1. A skier slides down a snowy slope, speeding up as she goes. Which energy transfer is taking place?**

A. Kinetic store to gravitational potential store
B. Thermal store to chemical store
C. Gravitational potential store to kinetic store
D. Elastic potential store to gravitational potential store

---

**2. A water wave has a frequency of 5 Hz and a wavelength of 3 m. What is the speed of the wave?**

A. 15 m/s
B. 8 m/s
C. 0.6 m/s
D. 1.7 m/s

---

**3. A car's engine produces a forward driving force of 800 N. Friction and air resistance together produce a backward force of 500 N. What is the resultant force on the car?**

A. 1300 N backwards
B. 500 N forwards
C. 800 N forwards
D. 300 N forwards

---

**4. A spacecraft is travelling through deep space with its engines switched off and no other forces acting on it. According to Newton's first law, what will happen to it?**

A. It will gradually slow down and stop
B. It will keep moving at the same speed in a straight line
C. It will speed up steadily
D. It will start to turn in a circle

---

**5. Which statement about energy is correct?**

A. Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred from one store to another
B. Energy is used up when a machine does work
C. Wasted energy is destroyed as it is transferred
D. Energy can be created by a moving object

---

**6. Which of these is an example of a longitudinal wave?**

A. Ripples on a pond
B. Light from the Sun
C. A wave on a rope shaken up and down
D. Sound travelling through air

---

**7. A trolley of mass 2 kg moves at a speed of 3 m/s. What is its kinetic energy? (Kinetic energy = 0.5 × mass × speed²)**

A. 6 J
B. 18 J
C. 9 J
D. 3 J

---

**8. A book rests on a table. Which statement correctly describes the forces on it?**

A. The weight of the book is bigger than the force from the table, so it presses down
B. The weight of the book is balanced by an equal upward force from the table, so the resultant force is zero
C. There are no forces acting on the book because it is not moving
D. The only force on the book is the force from the table

---

**9. What does the amplitude of a wave measure?**

A. The number of waves passing a point each second
B. The distance between two neighbouring peaks
C. The speed of the wave
D. The maximum distance a point on the wave moves from its rest position

---

**10. A cyclist stops pedalling on a flat road and gradually slows down. Which is the best explanation?**

A. Friction and air resistance act against her motion, so there is a resultant force in the opposite direction to her movement
B. A moving object always slows down when no force acts on it
C. Her kinetic energy is destroyed when she stops pedalling
D. There is no resultant force, so she slows down on her own

---
