**Topic: The digestive system (revision of organs and their jobs)**

## Section 2: Student worksheet (print or copy to the board)

### The digestive system

**Task 1: Starter (5 minutes). Silent, on your own.**
Write down the names of as many parts of the digestive system as you can remember, in the order food travels through them.

**Task 1b: Draw and label (15 minutes).**
Draw a large outline of the digestive system in your book (use the textbook diagram to help you). Label these parts:

mouth, oesophagus (food pipe), stomach, liver, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus

Use a ruler for label lines and pencil for the drawing.

**Task 3: The journey of a cheese sandwich (10 minutes).**
Write a paragraph (6 to 8 sentences) that describes the journey of a cheese sandwich through your digestive system. Start at the mouth and finish at the anus. Use at least **six** of the key words from Task 1b. Write in full sentences.

**Challenge (if you finish early).**
1. Why is the small intestine very long and folded up?
2. What would happen if the oesophagus was blocked?
3. Make up a quiz of 5 questions about the digestive system for a partner. Write the answers on the back.

## Section 3: Answers for the cover teacher

**Task 1 (order of the journey):** mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus. (The liver, gall bladder and pancreas are helper organs, so they are not on the main food pathway.)

**Task 2: Organ jobs (accept similar wording)**

| Organ | Its job |
|---|---|
| Mouth | Teeth chew and break food into smaller pieces. Saliva makes the food wet and easier to swallow. |
| Oesophagus | Carries food from the mouth to the stomach by muscles that squeeze behind the food. |
| Stomach | Churns the food and mixes it with acid and juices. The acid kills many germs. |
| Liver | Makes a green liquid called bile, which helps with the digestion of fats. |
| Small intestine | Finishes digesting food. Nutrients pass through the wall into the blood. |
| Large intestine | Absorbs water from what is left. |
| Rectum and anus | The rectum stores waste (faeces). The anus lets it leave the body. |

**Task 3: A good paragraph includes:** chewed in the mouth, swallowed, travels down the oesophagus, churned in the stomach, nutrients absorbed in the small intestine, water absorbed in the large intestine, waste leaves through the anus.

**Challenge answers**
1. Its length and folds give it a large surface area, so more nutrients can be absorbed into the blood.
2. Food couldn't get to the stomach, so the person couldn't eat properly. (Accept sensible answers.)

**End-of-lesson quiz (read aloud, students answer in their books)**
1. Which organ makes bile? *The liver.*
2. Where do most nutrients pass into the blood? *The small intestine.*
3. What does the large intestine absorb? *Water.*
4. What is the name of the food pipe? *The oesophagus.*
