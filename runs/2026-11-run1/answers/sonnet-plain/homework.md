# Year 8 French Homework: Hier, j'ai... (Yesterday, I...)

**Set:** this week | **Due:** next lesson | **Time:** about 30 minutes in total, split over two or three short sessions (10 to 15 minutes each is fine)

*Assumptions: students have just learned the perfect tense with avoir (j'ai + past participle) with regular -er verbs, and probably a few common ones such as -ir and -re verbs; they already know family words and opinion phrases (j'aime, je déteste, parce que). The homework mixes those together, and every task can be done and checked by the student alone, so a parent never needs to speak French.*

---

## Student sheet

### Your goal

Write and say what you and your family did yesterday, and give an opinion about it, in French.

### Words and phrases to help (use these, they're all from class)

| English | French |
|---|---|
| yesterday | hier |
| I played | j'ai joué |
| I watched | j'ai regardé |
| I listened to | j'ai écouté |
| I ate | j'ai mangé |
| I finished | j'ai fini |
| I sold | j'ai vendu |
| my mum / my dad | ma mère / mon père |
| my brother / my sister | mon frère / ma sœur |
| my grandma / my grandad | ma grand-mère / mon grand-père |
| I like / I don't like | j'aime / je n'aime pas |
| I hate | je déteste |
| because | parce que |
| it was fun / boring | c'était amusant / c'était ennuyeux |

**Reminder of the rule:** Take **j'ai** (or **il a / elle a / nous avons**), and add the past form of the verb. For -er verbs, change the **-er** ending to **-é**. Example: jouer becomes **j'ai joué**.

---

### Task 1: Match it up (5 minutes)

Match each French sentence to its English meaning. Write the letters in your book.

1. J'ai joué au football.
2. J'ai mangé une pizza.
3. J'ai regardé la télé.
4. J'ai écouté de la musique.
5. J'ai fini mes devoirs.

a) I finished my homework.
b) I listened to music.
c) I played football.
d) I watched TV.
e) I ate a pizza.

### Task 2: Fill in the gap (5 minutes)

Write the correct past form of the verb in brackets.

1. Hier, j'ai ________ (jouer) aux cartes avec mon frère.
2. Hier, j'ai ________ (regarder) un film avec ma mère.
3. Hier, j'ai ________ (manger) du chocolat.
4. Hier, j'ai ________ (écouter) mon père.
5. Hier, j'ai ________ (travailler) avec ma sœur.

### Task 3: Write your own (10 to 12 minutes)

Write **5 sentences** about what you and your family did yesterday (or last weekend). **For each, say who did it, what they did, and your opinion.** Use the pattern:

> Hier, j'ai [verb] avec [family member]. J'aime ça / Je n'aime pas ça parce que c'était [opinion].

Example:

> Hier, j'ai joué aux cartes avec ma sœur. J'aime ça parce que c'était amusant.

Try to include:

- at least **3 different verbs**
- at least **2 different family members**
- at least **1 opinion with parce que**

### Task 4: Say it aloud (5 minutes)

Read your five sentences out loud twice. Then record yourself reading them on a phone or tablet if you can, or just say them to a family member, a pet, or your reflection. Say them slowly and clearly.

**To help with pronunciation (for you and your family):** if you're unsure how a word sounds, use a free online dictionary with audio (for example WordReference or Larousse) or Google Translate's speaker button, and copy what you hear. Pay attention to the **-é** ending: **joué** sounds like "zhoo-AY", and the **j'ai** in front sounds like "zhay".

## Self-check checklist (students tick these off)

- [ ] Every sentence starts with **Hier** and has **j'ai** (or **il a / elle a**) before the verb.
- [ ] My verbs end in **-é** (for -er verbs).

## A note for parents and carers (no French needed!)

Thank you for supporting your child with homework. You don't need to speak any French to help. Here are three simple things you can do:

1. **Be the audience.** Ask your child to read their five sentences aloud to you. You don't need to understand them: just listen and say "Well done" or "Can you say it again more slowly?" Saying French aloud is a big part of learning it.
2. **Ask them to teach you.** Ask: "What does this one mean?" or "Can you tell me what you did yesterday in French?" Explaining to someone else is one of the best ways to learn.
3. **Use the answer sheet.** Your child can check Tasks 1 and 2 against the answers above. For Task 3, use the checklist together: you can tick the boxes just by looking (for example, do all the sentences start with "Hier"?). If anything doesn't match, ask your child to go back and fix it, or leave a note and they can ask me in class.

If your child gets really stuck, please don't worry. Write "I got stuck on..." and I will help in the next lesson. Please don't let homework turn into a battle: 30 minutes is plenty.

---

## Teacher notes

- **Marking:** Tasks 1 and 2 can be checked in class in 2 minutes; collect Task 3 and mark for accuracy of the perfect tense, use of *parce que* and variety of family members. Pick out common errors (forgetting j'ai, using the infinitive, forgetting the accent) for a starter next lesson.
- **Support:** students who find Task 3 difficult can write three sentences instead of five and use the example sentence as a frame. Stretch: add a second opinion with *je n'ai pas aimé* or *c'était génial*, or add *avec* plus a different person each time.
- **Common errors to watch for:** *j'ai jouer* (infinitive instead of past participle), missing accents, using *être* where *avoir* is needed, and dropping *ai*.
