## What we've been learning

**History: Romans in Britain**
We have travelled back in time to discover how the Romans invaded and settled in Britain. The children have learned about Roman soldiers, roads and towns, and how Roman life changed the country. Ask your child what they found most surprising!

**Science: Electricity**
In science we have been building simple circuits with batteries, bulbs, buzzers and switches. The children have learned how to make a circuit work and why a bulb won't light if there is a gap in the circuit. They have also been thinking about electricity safety.

**Maths**
We have been practising **column subtraction**, including exchanging (borrowing) across tens and hundreds. We have also focused on the **6, 7 and 9 times tables**. These can be tricky, so extra practice at home really helps.

**English: 'Escape from Pompeii'**
Our class book has been an exciting one! The children have enjoyed following the story and have been writing about the characters and the events. It links well with our history work, and many have asked to find out more about volcanoes.

## How you can help at home with times tables

Just five minutes a day is more effective than a long session once a week. Here are some ideas:

- Chant or sing the 6, 7 and 9 times tables together in the car or while cooking.
- Use the 9s finger trick, or notice that the digits in the 9 times table always add to 9 (18: 1 + 8 = 9).
- Play quick-fire questions, or games with dice or cards.
- Use a free online game such as Times Tables Rock Stars if your child's class has a login (ask us if you need the details).
- Mix up the order and ask the "missing number" questions (for example 7 x ? = 42), as the check will not always go in order.
