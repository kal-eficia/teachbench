# Feedback on your answer: "Explain why the Treaty of Versailles was so unpopular in Germany" (8 marks)

You have made a good start here. You have picked out several of the real terms of the Treaty and you have clearly learned some key facts. Here is what you did well and how to move it up to the next level.

## What you did well

- **You know the key terms.** Reparations, the loss of land, the army limit of 100,000 men and the war guilt clause are all correct and relevant. That's four solid points to build on.
- **You gave specific facts.** Naming Alsace-Lorraine and the army figure shows you have revised the detail. (One check: the reparations figure of £6.6 billion is right as the amount set in 1921, and it's worth saying when it was set, as that came after 1919.)
- **Your answer is clear and easy to follow.** Each sentence is about one point, and you answer the question about Germany's feelings.

## What to improve

The question says **"explain why"**, so the examiner wants to see you say *why each term made Germans angry*, not only what the terms were. At the moment your answer mostly lists the terms. Each fact is followed by "...and they were angry", which doesn't tell us why. This is the main reason the answer would sit in the middle of the mark range rather than the top.

**1. Explain each point, don't just state it.**
Right now you wrote: "Their army was cut to 100,000 men."
To explain, add a "because" or "this meant" that links it to how Germans felt:
> "Their army was cut to 100,000 men, which left Germany unable to defend itself and was humiliating for a proud military nation. Many Germans felt this treated them as a danger rather than an equal."

Use the same approach for each of your points:
- *Reparations:* Why was the amount such a problem? (It was huge compared to what Germany's economy could afford, and it led to hardship and later hyperinflation in 1923.)
- *Land:* What did losing land mean? (Germany lost around 10% of its territory and valuable industrial areas like the Saar coalfields, and millions of Germans found themselves living outside Germany, which damaged national pride and the economy.)
- *War guilt:* Why was Article 231 so upsetting? (Germans felt the blame was unfair, because they believed all countries shared responsibility, and it was used to justify the reparations.)

**2. Add the "diktat" idea.**
One of the biggest reasons for anger was that Germany wasn't allowed to take part in the negotiations and had to sign or face invasion. Germans called it a **"diktat"**, a dictated peace. This is an important reason, and it will make your answer stand out.

**3. Link the points together.**
At the moment each sentence stands alone. Try connecting ideas: "This was made worse by...", "As a result...", "Together, these terms made Germans feel...". You could also decide which reason mattered *most* and say why. That moves you from listing to building an argument.

**4. Write a proper conclusion.**
"This is why it was unpopular" repeats the question. Instead, finish with a sentence that sums up the main reason: "Overall, Germans hated the Treaty most because it was imposed on them without discussion and blamed them for a war they didn't believe they alone caused, while the terms crippled their economy and army."

**5. Use the right amount of detail for 8 marks.**
Aim for three or four paragraphs, each one with: **Point** (a term), **Evidence** (a fact), **Explain** (why this made Germans angry) and **Link** (back to the question). Your facts are already there, so you need to add the explaining.
