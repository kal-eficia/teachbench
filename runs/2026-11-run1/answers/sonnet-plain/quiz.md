# Year 7 Geography: Rivers Check-In Quiz

*Assumptions: about 15 minutes, no notes, low-stakes. Questions follow the order of the unit (source to mouth, erosion, transportation, deposition, landforms, flooding). They are recall questions that don't need a map.*

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ______________

Circle **one** letter for each question.

---

**1. What is the source of a river?**

A. The place where the river flows into the sea
B. The place where the river begins, often in hills or mountains
C. The place where two rivers meet
D. The widest part of the river

**2. What is the mouth of a river?**

A. The highest point, where the water starts to flow
B. The narrowest and shallowest part of the river
C. The place where a smaller stream joins the river
D. The place where the river flows into the sea or a lake

**3. What is a tributary?**

A. A smaller stream or river that flows into a larger river
B. The strip of land on either side of a river
C. The whole area of land that a river collects its water from
D. The point where a river begins

**4. What does erosion mean?**

A. Rock and soil being carried along by the river
B. The river dropping the material it is carrying
C. The river wearing away its bed and banks
D. The river freezing in cold weather

**5. Rocks carried by a river knock into each other and become smaller and rounder. What is this process called?**

A. Attrition
B. Hydraulic action
C. Solution
D. Abrasion

**6. Large rocks and boulders are rolled along the bed of a river. What is this type of transportation called?**

A. Suspension
B. Solution
C. Saltation
D. Traction

**7. When is a river most likely to deposit (drop) the material it is carrying?**

A. When it speeds up after heavy rain
B. When it loses energy and slows down
C. When it flows down a steep slope
D. When it flows over hard rock

**8. What is a meander?**

A. A steep drop where water falls from a height
B. A long straight stretch of river in the mountains
C. A large bend or curve in a river
D. A lake formed when a river is blocked

**9. The water flows fastest on the outside of a meander bend. What landform does erosion create there?**

A. A slip-off slope
B. An oxbow lake
C. A delta
D. A river cliff

**10. How does a waterfall form?**

A. A river flows over a layer of hard rock on top of softer rock, and the softer rock wears away faster
B. A river freezes in winter and the ice cracks the rock into a step
C. A river drops its load in a pile and the pile grows into a high wall
D. A river flows over soft rock on top of hard rock, so the hard rock wears away faster

**11. Which of these is most likely to cause a river to flood?**

A. A long, dry summer
B. Heavy rain falling for several days on ground that is already soaked
C. Planting lots of trees on the hillsides
D. A small drop in the amount of rainfall

**12. Which of these would help to reduce the risk of flooding?**

A. Cutting down forests in the upper course
B. Building new houses on the floodplain
C. Building flood walls or embankments along the river banks
D. Removing all the plants from the river banks
