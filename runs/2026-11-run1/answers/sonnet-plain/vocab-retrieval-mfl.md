# Year 7 Spanish: 10-Minute Retrieval Starter (10 lessons over two weeks)

**Assumptions:** you see the class five times a week, so there are 10 starters. If you teach fewer lessons, use them in order and carry on later. Students have mini-whiteboards or the back of their books. No notes are allowed, because the point is to pull the words out of memory.

## The routine (same every day, so no explaining is needed)

| Minutes | Part | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 1:00 | **Settle** | Slide on screen, students begin straight away |
| 1:00 to 5:00 | **Task 1: Brain dump or quick write** | Silent, no notes. Work from memory. |
| 5:00 to 8:00 | **Task 2: Say it** | Pair talk or whole-class oral drill |
| 8:00 to 10:00 | **Check and fix** | Show answers. Students correct in a different colour. Note the 2 most common errors and re-teach in 30 seconds. |

**Why this works:** retrieval from memory (not copying or re-reading), spacing (each topic returns several times), mixing topics together, and quick feedback.

**Tip:** keep a tally of the most-missed words. Those become the first items in the next day's starter.

## Week 1

### Day 1: Greetings and numbers
- **Task 1:** Write 5 greetings or goodbyes, then the numbers 1 to 10 in Spanish.
- **Task 2:** Pairs: greet each other and ask and answer "¿Cómo estás?" and "¿Cómo te llamas?"
- **Check:** Watch for missing accents and spelling of *seis*, *siete*, *diez*.

### Day 2: School subjects
- **Task 1:** Write every school subject you can remember in Spanish. How many can you get in 90 seconds? Then add the English meaning.
- **Task 2:** Teacher says the English; students call out the Spanish (chorally).
- **Check:** *el inglés*, *la historia*, *las matemáticas*, *la informática*.

### Day 3: Family
- **Task 1:** Draw a simple family tree and label the members in Spanish.
- **Task 2:** Pairs: "Tengo un hermano / una hermana..." Say who is in your family.
- **Check:** Gender (*mi padre*, *mi madre*) and plurals (*mis hermanos*).

### Day 4: Opinions
- **Task 1:** Complete 6 sentences: *Me gusta... / No me gusta...* + a school subject.
- **Task 2:** Teacher calls a subject; students stand if they like it and sit if not, and say the sentence aloud.
- **Check:** *me gusta* vs *no me gusta*; both stay singular with a subject.

### Day 5: Numbers 1 to 31, mixed with greetings
- **Task 1:** Write the numbers: 12, 15, 16, 19, 21, 27, 30, 31 in Spanish. Then write the answer to 10 + 5, 20 + 7, 31 - 4 in Spanish.
- **Task 2:** "Beat the teacher" counting: the class counts round the room, skipping every third number.
- **Check:** The tricky ones: *dieciséis*, *veintiuno*, *treinta y uno*.

---

## Week 2

### Day 6: Spot the mistake
- **Task 1:** Show 8 sentences on screen, each containing one error from the earlier topics (wrong gender, missing *porque*, wrong number spelling). Students find and fix them.
- **Task 2:** Pairs explain the correction aloud in English.
- **Check:** Ask which errors seemed most common and why.

### Day 7: Opinions with reasons
- **Task 1:** Write 4 full sentences: *Me gusta el inglés porque es divertido.* Use *me gusta*, *no me gusta*, *porque es* and a different adjective each time.
- **Task 2:** Pairs swap and ask each other: "¿Te gusta...? ¿Por qué?"
- **Check:** Adjective list: *divertido*, *aburrido*, *difícil*, *fácil*, *interesante*, *útil*.

### Day 8: Family and numbers combined
- **Task 1:** Write 3 sentences about a family member, including their age: *Mi hermano tiene doce años.* (Use numbers up to 31.)
- **Task 2:** Pairs interview each other: "¿Cuántos años tienes?" "¿Tienes hermanos?"
- **Check:** *tiene* (he/she has) vs *tengo* (I have).

### Day 9: Mixed topics quiz
- **Task 1:** 12-question quiz mixing every topic. Give 3 questions on each of greetings, numbers, subjects and family (no new vocabulary). Students write answers.
- **Task 2:** Swap and check, or answer on mini-whiteboards.
- **Check:** Record the 3 questions the class missed most.

### Day 10: Free recall and extension
- **Task 1:** "Write everything you know." Give 4 minutes to write as much Spanish as possible from any topic, with a challenge of using at least one connective (*y*, *pero*, *porque*).
- **Task 2:** Pairs: share your best sentence. The class votes on the best three.
- **Check:** Celebrate improvement. Use this as a snapshot of what needs revisiting.

---

## Making it work

- **Vary the questioning:** cold-call students at random, as well as taking volunteers.
- **Low stakes:** it is for learning, not for grades. Students correct their own work in a different colour.
- **Differentiate:** support students with a sentence starter (e.g. *Me gusta ___ porque es ___*); stretch students with *también* or *pero*.
- **Keep a running list** of the words students keep forgetting and put them first the next day.
- **Next steps:** after two weeks, repeat the cycle with fewer prompts, and add new topics as you teach them.
