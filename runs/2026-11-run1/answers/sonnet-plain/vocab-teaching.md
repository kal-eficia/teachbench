# Teaching key terms so they stick (Year 10 RS, AQA Christianity and Islam)

**Assumptions:** AQA GCSE Religious Studies A, Christianity and Islam as the two beliefs and teachings units. You have around 6 to 8 weeks before a mock, and short bursts of a few minutes in many lessons. You have not told me whether students lose marks from not using the terms at all, or from using them loosely, so this plan treats both.

## Why marks are lost
Typical problems on AQA:
- Using the word without meaning it: "Muslims believe in tawhid because they believe in Allah."
- Describing the idea without naming it ("God coming as a man" instead of *incarnation*).
- Mixing similar terms: atonement vs salvation, risalah vs prophethood, akhirah vs judgement.
- Using the term in the wrong place, so evaluation or 5 and 12 mark answers read as "beliefs listed" rather than analysed.

AQA mark schemes reward accurate use of **specialist vocabulary**, particularly in the 4, 5 and 12 mark questions. In 12 markers, accurate vocabulary supports the "reasoned judgement" and the "developed" mark bands.

## Principle: teach each word as a small bundle
For every term, students learn five things:
1. **Say it:** correct pronunciation and spelling.
2. **Define it:** a short, accurate definition they can write in a sentence.
3. **Link it:** how it connects to other beliefs.
4. **Example it:** a quotation or a practice.
5. **Use it:** in a sentence and in an exam-style answer.

## The six terms

| Term | Meaning (student-ready) | Key link | Tempting confusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tawhid** | The oneness of Allah: Allah is one, unique, with no partners | Shirk (opposite); Surah 112; basis of the Shahadah | "Belief in Allah" (too vague) |
| **Akhirah** | Life after death: the Day of Judgement, heaven and hell | Linked to Allah's justice and Muslims' actions in this life | "Just heaven" or "just judgement" |
| **Risalah** | Prophethood: Allah's guidance revealed through messengers, ending with Muhammad | Prophets (Ibrahim, Isa, Muhammad); Qur'an | Mixing with "revelation" or "angels" |
| **Incarnation** | God becoming human in Jesus | John 1:14; Trinity; Jesus as Son | "Resurrection" or "birth" |
| **Atonement** | The reconciling of humans with God through Jesus' death, which overcame sin | Crucifixion, salvation, grace | "Forgiveness" only; "sacrifice" only |
| **The Trinity** | One God in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit | Nicene Creed; baptism of Jesus; incarnation | "Three gods" |

Note for quality: check your exam board's specification wording and your own textbook definitions so students use the phrases the mark scheme expects. For example, AQA uses "the oneness of Allah" for tawhid and "the idea of life after death" for akhirah.

## The plan: four phases over several weeks

### Phase 1: First teach (1 lesson per pair of terms)
- Start with the **story or image** (for example, Surah 112 for tawhid, Genesis 1 and John 1 for incarnation and Trinity, the Day of Judgement imagery for akhirah).
- Use a **word map**: term in the middle; definition, example, opposite or confusion, link.
- Teach **the confusion** deliberately: "Why is 'three gods' wrong? What do Christians mean by 'persons'?" Misconceptions that are explained stick better than ones that are ignored.
- Have students rephrase the definition in their own words, then compare to a model definition.

### Phase 2: Retrieval practice (starters and plenaries, 5 minutes)
Spaced and low-stakes. Give one question per term, returning to older terms often.
- **Brain dump:** "Write everything you know about the Trinity in 90 seconds."
- **Definition recall:** show the definition, students name the term, and the reverse.
- **True / false with fix:** "Tawhid means Muslims believe in many prophets." Students correct it.
- **Odd one out:** *Trinity, incarnation, risalah, atonement.* Which isn't a Christian belief? Why?
- Plan the sequence so each term returns at roughly 1 day, 1 week and 3 weeks after teaching.

### Phase 3: Use the words in sentences (the main fix)
Students need to *use* the terms, not just define them.
- **Sentence frames:**
  - "Muslims believe in tawhid, which means ..., so they ..."
  - "The incarnation is important to Christians because ..., which shows ..."
  - "Atonement is significant because without it ..."
- **Upgrade the sentence:** give a weak answer ("Christians believe in the Trinity because it says so in the Bible") and have students rewrite it using the term accurately.
- **Connect two terms:** "Explain how the incarnation links to atonement." "How does tawhid affect Muslim attitudes to the afterlife (akhirah)?"
- **Linking grid:** a 6 by 6 grid where students write how each pair of terms connects. This also pushes them towards the analysis AQA wants.

### Phase 4: Exam-embedding
- Use short **4 mark** (two developed points with a source) and **5 mark** questions, and a plan for **12 markers** that requires at least two key terms used accurately.
- **Mark it for vocabulary only:** give students a model answer and ask them to highlight each key term and judge whether it is used correctly. Then they do the same for a peer's answer.
- **Traffic-light their own answers:** green for correct use, amber for a term used vaguely, red for a missing term.
- Give a **"banned phrases"** list for the lesson: "basically", "God thing", "believe in God" (use the term instead).

## Activities that work well

1. **Vocabulary ladder:** each student has a card of the six terms and rates themselves: *heard of it / can define it / can use it in a sentence / can use it in an evaluation*. Revisit weekly.
2. **Explain to a Year 7:** students record or write a two-minute explanation for a younger pupil, without jargon, then add the correct term.
3. **Missing word cloze:** a model answer with the terms removed. Students fill in and justify.
4. **Odd definitions:** show a plausible but wrong definition and ask why it loses marks (e.g. "atonement means God forgave everyone").
5. **Quick quizzes:** low-stakes, five questions, same terms in new contexts.
6. **Quotation pairing:** match each term to a source of wisdom and authority, such as Surah 112 for tawhid, John 1:14 for incarnation, Matthew 28:19 for the Trinity, and so on. Check these against your scheme of work.

## Spelling and pronunciation
- Spell on the board in chunks: *ta-wheed*, *ak-hi-rah*, *ri-sa-lah*.
- Say each aloud as a class, then in pairs.
- Include a short "spell it" check in the retrieval starter. Marks are rarely lost for minor transliteration, but clear, consistent spelling helps students feel confident using them.

## Assessment and checking
- **Weekly:** 6-question vocabulary quiz (mix of define, use in a sentence, spot the error).
- **Fortnightly:** one short exam question where the focus is vocabulary.
- **Track:** who still avoids each term, who uses it vaguely, who uses it well. Target the second group with explicit feedback.
- **Feedback comment bank:** "Name the belief: this is the idea of ...", "You've described it, now use the key term", "Link this to ... to develop."

## Common issues to watch
- **Overgeneralising "Muslims believe" or "Christians believe."** Teach students to say "many Christians", "some Christians", "Sunni Muslims" where appropriate. AQA values awareness of diversity of belief.
- **Atonement:** make sure students know that Christians hold different understandings of how it works. Don't let them reduce it to "Jesus died for our sins" without explaining why that matters.
- **Trinity:** students often write "three gods". Insist on "one God in three persons".
- **Risalah:** make clear it covers the whole idea of prophets and messengers, not just Muhammad.

## Simple one-page summary for students

| Term | One-line definition | Use it like this |
|---|---|---|
| Tawhid | The oneness of Allah | "Because of tawhid, Muslims reject shirk." |
| Akhirah | Life after death and the Day of Judgement | "Belief in akhirah encourages Muslims to ..." |
| Risalah | Prophethood and revelation through messengers | "Through risalah, Allah guides humanity." |
| Incarnation | God becoming human in Jesus | "The incarnation shows God's love and closeness." |
| Atonement | Restoring the relationship between humans and God through Jesus' death | "Atonement matters because it offers salvation." |
| The Trinity | One God in three persons | "The Trinity shows God as Father, Son and Holy Spirit." |

Check these against your own specification and textbook so the wording matches what your students will be marked against.
