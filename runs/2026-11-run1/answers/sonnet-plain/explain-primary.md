# Why do we have day and night?

*A short explanation to read aloud to Year 1 (ages 5 and 6), with actions the children can do.*

*Assumption: you have a torch and a globe or a ball, but the explanation works without them.*

---

## What you need (optional)

- A torch (the Sun)
- A globe or a ball with a sticker on it (the Earth, with the sticker as "us")

---

## Read aloud

Boys and girls, I have a question for you. When you wake up in the morning, is it light or dark?

*(Let them answer.)*

And when you go to bed, is it light or dark?

*(Let them answer.)*

So why is it light in the day and dark at night? Let's find out!

**Meet the Sun.**
Up in the sky, there is a great big ball of fire called the **Sun**. It is very, very far away. The Sun gives us light and keeps us warm.

*(Hold up the torch.)*

**Meet the Earth.**
We live on a giant ball called the **Earth**. Put your hands in a big circle. That's the Earth! You are living on it right now.

*(Hold up the globe.)*

**The Earth spins.**
Here's the clever part. The Earth is always turning round, just like a spinning top. Let's all spin slowly, very slowly, on the spot! Wheee!

*(Children spin gently, or turn on the spot.)*

It spins so slowly that we can't feel it. It takes one whole day to go all the way round.

**When it's day.**
The side of the Earth that faces the Sun gets the Sun's light. Look at the sticker. The sticker is facing the torch, so it's **daytime**! The Sun is shining on us.

*(Shine the torch on the sticker side of the globe.)*

**When it's night.**
Now let's turn the Earth slowly. The sticker is turning away from the torch. The light can't reach it. It's getting dark. It's **night-time**. The Sun hasn't gone anywhere. We have just turned away from it.

*(Turn the globe slowly until the sticker is in shadow.)*

**And the morning comes.**
Keep turning, and soon the sticker turns back to face the torch. The light is back. It's **morning**! The Sun didn't move at all. It was our Earth that was spinning.

---

## Say it together

*Do the actions as you chant.*

> "The Sun shines bright, it's day (arms up high).
> The Earth turns round, it's night (curl up small).
> The Earth keeps spinning, round and round (spin),
> And morning comes again!" (jump up)

---

## Quick questions to check

1. What gives us light in the day? *(The Sun.)*
2. What does the Earth do? *(It spins.)*
3. What do we call it when our part of the Earth is facing away from the Sun? *(Night.)*
4. Does the Sun go to bed? *(No. We turn away from it.)*

---

## Safety note

Remind the children **never** to look straight at the Sun, because it can hurt their eyes.

---

## Follow-up ideas

- Draw a picture of something you do in the day and something you do at night.
- Sort pictures into "day" and "night" (sun, moon, stars, breakfast, bedtime).
- Play "Sun and Earth" outside: one child holds the torch, one slowly turns.
