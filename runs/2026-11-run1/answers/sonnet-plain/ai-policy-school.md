# Student AI Use Policy

**Applies to:** all students, ages 11 to 18 | **Review date:** [insert, suggest each September]

## Our approach

AI tools can be a powerful way to learn, but only if you are still the one doing the thinking. Our rule of thumb: **use AI to help you understand; never use it to do the work you are being assessed on.** If you aren't sure whether something is allowed, ask your teacher first.

## 1. Using AI to learn (encouraged)

Where your teacher has not said otherwise, you may use AI to:
- Explain a topic in a different way, or break it into smaller steps
- Quiz you or make practice questions and flashcards
- Give feedback on work you have already written, so you can improve it yourself
- Help you plan revision, or brainstorm ideas before you start
- Check your understanding by asking it to look for gaps in what you know

**A good test:** after using AI, could you explain the idea to someone else without it?

## 2. Using AI to cheat (not allowed)

You must not:
- Submit AI-written or AI-edited work (including text, code, images, or answers) as your own
- Use AI in tests, exams, or any assessment, or in homework where your teacher has said "no AI"
- Use AI to paraphrase or disguise copied work
- Make up sources or quotes. AI often invents facts, so check anything you use.

**Where AI is allowed in an assignment, you must say so.** Add a short note: what tool you used, what you asked, and what you did with the answer. Teachers may ask you to explain your work or complete part of it in class.

Breaking these rules is treated as **academic misconduct** and follows our usual behaviour and exam board procedures. Exam boards (JCQ) can penalise or disqualify students.

## 3. Staying safe

- **Never share personal information.** That includes your full name, address, school, photos, passwords, or anything about other people.
- **Don't upload other people's work, photos or details** without their permission.
- **Never use AI to make fake, harmful or hurtful content** about anyone, including images, voices, videos, or messages. This includes jokes. It is bullying and may be illegal.
- **Be careful what you trust.** AI can be wrong, biased or out of date. Check important facts with a reliable source or a teacher.
- **Check the age rules.** Many AI tools require users to be 13 or over, or 18 with parental consent. Use only tools your school has approved, or that are suitable for your age.
- **Don't rely on AI for personal, emotional or health problems.** Talk to a trusted adult, teacher or the school's pastoral team.
- **If something upsets or worries you,** tell a member of staff straight away.

## 4. Year group guidance

| Years | What this looks like |
|---|---|
| **7 to 9** | Use only AI tools approved by the school, under teacher guidance. |
| **10 to 11** | May use AI for revision and feedback. Coursework and NEA follow exam board rules. |
| **12 to 13** | More independence, with full responsibility to declare AI use and check facts. |

## 5. Responsibilities

- **Students:** follow this policy, be honest, and ask if unsure.
- **Teachers:** say clearly what is allowed for each task, and talk through AI use with students.
- **Parents/carers:** support these expectations and talk to your child about safe use.
- **School:** keep this policy updated and provide guidance on using AI well.
